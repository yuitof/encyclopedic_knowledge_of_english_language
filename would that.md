would that
: wish (by following it with subjunctive mood)

- Break, break, break, On they cold gray stones, O Sea! And I *would that* my tongue could utter The thoughts that arise in me.

also see: [[O! for]]
- And the stately ships goon To their haven under the hill; But *O for* the touch of a vanish'd hand, And the sound of a voice that is still!
