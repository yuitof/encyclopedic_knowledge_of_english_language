A place to explore ideas and the connections between them. **Knowledge Commons** is a small, growing encyclopedia for mathematics, computing, language, and the habits of academic study.

These are original demonstration articles. No notes from the existing Obsidian vault have been selected for publication.

## Follow the connections

Every article is part of a network. Follow blue links to related ideas, use the topic explorer to browse, and look to the right for backlinks showing which articles refer to the current one.

> [!tip] Find an idea
> Type a word or phrase into the search field. Search looks inside articles as well as their titles. Use the arrow keys and Enter to open a result, or press Escape to leave search.

## Read in your own way

The article contents stay beside the text on larger screens. On a phone, use **Contents & topics** above the article. The appearance control lets you switch between light and dark reading.
