A useful **study note** explains an idea in the writer’s own words, records the grounds for the explanation, and connects it to ideas that help a reader understand it.

## Questions before conclusions

Begin with a question that can be answered. Separate definitions, observations, and interpretations. Record what would change your mind.

- What is the claim?
- Which assumptions does it need?
- What evidence supports it?
- Where does it connect to an existing explanation?
