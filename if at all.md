To add to other responses, you’ll see a similar phrase “if that.” It means “maybe not even that,” where “that” stands for something earlier in the sentence.

“You can try to sell your bike, but you’ll only get about $50, if that.” Here “that” refers to $50, so “if that” means “you may not get as much as $50.”

You also see “if any,” which is similar to “If at all.” You pick the form that matches the thing you are talking about.
