close-hauled

: **Close-hauled** is a point of sail where a boat is oriented as close to the wind as possible, typically at an angle of 30 to 45 degrees off the wind direction. Sailing close-hauled allows a vessel to make progress upwind through a combination of precise sail trim and keel or centerboard resistance, creating lift that propels the boat forward while minimizing sideways drift.
