# Encyclopedic Knowledge of English Language

An evolving encyclopedia of the English language.

This repository is where I collect what I discover about English—whether it
concerns a word, an expression, grammar, usage, history, or culture. The notes
are not limited to a single subject or format; together, they form a growing
record of the language and the ideas surrounding it.

## What you will find here

- Words, phrases, and their meanings
- Grammar and usage notes
- Cultural and historical context
- Connections between related ideas
- Other discoveries about the English language

This is a living collection, so its contents will continue to grow and change
as I learn.

## Website development

A Quartz 5 website for an explicitly selected public collection lives in [`site/`](site/README.md). Its Wikipedia-inspired interface includes full-text client search, topic navigation, article contents, backlinks and mathematical typesetting.

The original collection is organized under [`vault/`](vault/): Markdown in `notes/`, images in `assets/`, and settings in `.obsidian/`. Open **`vault/`** as the Obsidian vault. New files are **not automatically selected for the website**; the reviewed publishing manifest selects notes and their referenced images. Publishing controls apply to generated Pages files, while this source repository and its history still contain the vault.

Read [`site/README.md`](site/README.md) for setup, tests and automatic GitHub Pages deployment on pushes to `main`, and [`site/docs/publication.md`](site/docs/publication.md) before approving notes or attachments. No website deployment is performed automatically by the experimental feature branch.

The reading interface also offers persistent text/width/color controls, a newest-modified-first sidebar, a generated article listing, and a configurable selection of real-note snippets on the main page.
