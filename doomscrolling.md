The behavior is rooted in our brain's limbic system - often referred to as the [[lizard]] or reptilian brain - that's dominated by a structure called the amygdala. It promotes self-preservation and drives the fight-or-flight response to danger, [[fuel|fueling]] us to [[troll for]] threats.

"Stress [[stoke|stokes]] our primary urge to scroll," she says. "We're hypervigilant and scanning for danger. The more you scroll, the more you feel you need to."
