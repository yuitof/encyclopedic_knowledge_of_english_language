**before long** (/bɪˈfɔːr lɔːŋ/) is an expression meaning soon or after a short time. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/before%20long))

## Examples

A narrator describes how someone's attention shifts during a power outage.

> At first, Rachel was mostly annoyed about the power outage, but before long, her attention shifted to the front door.
