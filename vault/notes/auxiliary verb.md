**auxiliary verb** (/ɔːɡˈzɪliəri vɜːb/) is a verb used with a main verb to form constructions such as questions, negatives, and [[tense|tenses]].

It is often abbreviated **aux.** or **AUX** in grammatical notation.

## Examples

Dictionary example.

> In the question ‘Do you know where he has gone?’, ‘do’ and ‘has’ are auxiliaries.
