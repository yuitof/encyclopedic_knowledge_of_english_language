take the edge off
: to make something unpleasant have less of an effect on someone
- But I could fire you, if that would [[take the edge off]].

- *Well, this will [[put down X on Y|take the edge off]], and I still have money to put down on a room,* Danny thought.
これで edge が減るだろう。edge はこの場合、クスリ切れの辛さのこと。「これで少しは楽になる」
