**out of pocket** (/ˌaʊt əv ˈpɒkɪt/) is an expression whose meaning depends on context: unavailable in some workplace usage, or wildly inappropriate in [[slang]].

These uses differ from the financial sense of paying with your own money.

## Examples

The Stack Exchange question reports workplace usage. This example concerns availability.

> The phrase "out of pocket" is often used in my office to mean "unavailable".
