almost
: 

almost + 動詞の過去形 で「しそうになった」っていうことを言える。数量や程度の前で使うことはよく覚えてそうだけど、この使い方も使いやすそう


almost が同等比較の前に置かれる例 
- Now I was being driven around the Catalan countryside at 100 miles per hour in a rally car. It's almost as ridiculous as still not being subscribed to the channel or liking this video yet. We're trying to reach 10 million subscribers so subscribe and like the video now. Otherwise I'll get Ernie to run you over.
- Housing has become almost as big a problem as education.
