**blow this Popsicle stand** (/bloʊ ðɪs ˈpɑːpsɪkəl stænd/) is an informal, humorous phrase meaning to leave a place [[quick|quickly]].

## Examples

Context: Calliope uses the expression while leaving an area in *Elden Ring*.

> Time to blow this Popsicle stand.
