**rhino** (/ˈraɪnəʊ/) is an informal word for a **rhinoceros**: a large, heavy animal with thick skin and one or two horns on its nose, native to Africa or Asia. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/rhino))

Countable noun; plural **rhinos**.
