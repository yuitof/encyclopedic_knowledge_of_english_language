**come** (/kʌm/) is an intransitive verb meaning to move towards, reach, arrive at, or accompany someone to a place. In the present continuous, **I'm coming** commonly means “I'm on my way” or “I'm joining you.”

## Examples

When Padmé speaks fatalistically before a dangerous mission, Obi-Wan reassures her that he will accompany her:

> "I'm coming with you."
