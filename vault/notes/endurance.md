**Endurance** (/ɪnˈdʊrəns/) is the name of the main spacecraft in *Interstellar* (2014), used for the crew’s voyage through [[space]].

Source: [AvatarLabs — Interstellar: Endurance Exploration](https://awards.avatarlabs.com/2014/interstellar/endurance.html), created with Warner Bros., Paramount Pictures, and the film’s production team.

The ordinary noun **endurance** means the ability to keep going through hardship or prolonged difficulty. The spacecraft’s name can be read as suggesting this ability to persist and survive.

Dictionary: [Merriam-Webster — endurance](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/endurance).
