**flip phone** (/ˈflɪp foʊn/) is a mobile phone that folds shut, with one section opening away from the other. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/flip%20phone))

## Examples

During an Omegle [[scavenger hunt]], a player must find an older folding phone.

> You have 5 minutes, [[by the way]], to find a flip phone, brother.
