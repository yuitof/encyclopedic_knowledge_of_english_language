**like what?** (/laɪk ˈwɒt/) is an informal question asking someone to give a specific example. Here, **like** means **“for example.”**

Source: [Cambridge English Grammar Today — Like, “Asking for an example (Like what?)”](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/like-verb).

## Examples

Cambridge gives an exchange in which one speaker asks for details:

> **A:** Some really funny things happened on the last day of school.
>
> **B:** Funny things? Like what?
