**water cannon** (/ˈwɔːtə kænən/) is a machine that sprays a powerful jet of water, used by police to control crowds.

## Pronunciation

**Cannon** and **canon** are [[homophone|homophones]]: both are pronounced /ˈkænən/, with stress on the first syllable (**CAN**-uhn). The extra **n** in **cannon** does not change its pronunciation. **Water cannon** adds **water** before that same sound.

## Examples

Reporting on protests in France, the BBC describes police dispersing demonstrators. Excerpt from a longer sentence:

> … police fired tear gas and water cannon to disperse protesters in a number of cities including Paris, …

— [BBC News — protests in France, 25 October 2026](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c95ynd587ynzo).
