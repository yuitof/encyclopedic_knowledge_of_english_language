**sleep call** (/ˈsliːp kɔːl/) is an informal online term for a phone or voice call that stays connected while one or both participants fall asleep. It can [[provide]] a sense of company, especially for people who are apart.

Source: [CNN Indonesia — Tren Sleepcall, Telepon hingga Tertidur](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/gaya-hidup/20211116103742-277-721734/tren-sleepcall-telepon-hingga-tertidur).

## Examples

The speaker is reading prices for online companionship services:

> I can pay $40 for a sleep call, chat.

— [I Tried Buying an Online Girlfriend, around 3:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvNvMc8c2Go&t=201s).
