**Lyrical Ballads** is the poetry collection by William Wordsworth and Samuel Taylor Coleridge first published in 1798. It helped shape English Romanticism through its interest in ordinary speech and experience.

Wordsworth’s influential preface first appeared in the 1800 edition. Related: [[Dove Cottage]].
