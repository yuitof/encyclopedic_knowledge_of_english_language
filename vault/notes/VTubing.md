**VTubing** (/ˈviːtjuːbɪŋ/) is the activity of creating videos or livestreams as a **VTuber**, using a virtual avatar to represent the performer. It can name the activity as an uncountable noun or function as the **-ing** form of the informal verb **VTube**. ([Wiktionary: VTubing](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/VTubing))

## Word formation and grammar

Turning a noun into a verb is called **conversion**, or informally **verbing**. [[conjugation|Conjugation]] means forming the grammatical forms of a verb after it exists. These are different steps. ([Cambridge: word formation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-formation_2))

The history need not be a simple chain from *YouTube* to *VTube* to *VTubing*: Wiktionary analyzes *VTubing* as a blend of *VTuber* and *YouTubing*. Its role in a sentence shows whether it is naming an activity or forming part of a verb phrase.

**YouTube can also be a verb.** Dictionary.com records *YouTube*, *YouTubed*, and *YouTubing*, meaning to post, view, or share videos on YouTube. Both *VTube → VTubing* and *YouTube → YouTubing* drop the final silent **e** before **-ing**. ([Dictionary.com: YouTube](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/youtube))

*VTubing* specifically identifies performing through an avatar; *YouTubing* identifies activity associated with a platform. Their existence is established, but these sources do not establish that one is more frequent or more likely to be used as a verb.

## Examples

A participant explains that experience with creating content through a virtual avatar may help identify the real VTuber. Here, *VTubing* names an activity after *do a little*.

> I do a little VTubing myself.

— [1 VTUBER vs 5 FAKES | GUESS THE VTUBER (VR) — 1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWvZMXckc74&t=78s)

Someone then asks a contestant about their performer name. Here, *you're VTubing* means *you are performing as a VTuber*: **are + -ing** is a progressive verb construction.

> Do you have like a VTuber name? Like something you go by when you're VTubing?
