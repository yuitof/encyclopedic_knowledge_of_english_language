**BLEACH** (/bliːtʃ/) is a Japanese supernatural action anime based on Tite Kubo’s manga of the same name. The original Studio Pierrot series aired in Japan from 2004 to 2012 and ran for 366 episodes. [VIZ Media](https://www.viz.com/bleach) · [Studio Pierrot](https://en.pierrot.jp/archive/2000/tv00_20.html)

The story follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who can see ghosts. After receiving powers from Rukia Kuchiki, he becomes a substitute Soul Reaper and protects the living from hostile spirits called **Hollows**. [VIZ Media synopsis](https://cpg.viz.com/brands/bleach)

[[Yoruichi Shihōin]], a former Soul Reaper captain, is one of Ichigo’s allies. [Official character guide](https://bleach-anime.com/en/character/?chara=14)

**BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War** continues the story and adapts the manga’s final arc. [VIZ Media](https://www.viz.com/press-releases/v/1006765)
