**spin (someone) around** (/spɪn əˈraʊnd/) is a phrasal verb meaning to turn quickly, or make someone turn quickly, to face the opposite direction. **Someone** is optional: you can turn yourself or turn another person. The past [[tense]] is **spun**.

Source: [Cambridge — spin (someone) around](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/spin-around).

## Examples

Cambridge describes someone turning to investigate:

> She spun around to see what had happened.
