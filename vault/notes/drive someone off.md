**drive someone off** (/draɪv ˈsʌmwʌn ɒf/) is a separable transitive phrasal verb meaning to force someone or something to move back or go away. The object can come between **drive** and **off**, as in **drive someone off**.

Source: [Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary — drive off](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/drive-off).
