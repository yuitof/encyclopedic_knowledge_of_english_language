**Robert Burns** (1759–1796) was a Scottish poet and songwriter who wrote in Scots and English. His works include “To a Mouse,” “Tam o’ Shanter,” and “Auld Lang Syne.”

Source: [Wikipedia — Robert Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Burns).
