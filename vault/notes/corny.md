**corny** (/ˈkɔːrni/) is an informal adjective for something that feels [[cheesy]], often because it's overly sentimental or too familiar to feel original. It can be used playfully or critically. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/corny)

In the context of online friends, calling the idea of having friends online **corny** suggests that the speaker sees it as uncool or [[wack and lame|lame]].

## Examples

After a virtual Olympics event, the narrator admits the whole thing felt cheesy but says it helped him get through the day.

> The whole thing was pretty corny, but I don't care.
