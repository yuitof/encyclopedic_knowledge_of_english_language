**muscle memory** (/ˈmʌsl ˈmeməri/) is the ability to repeat a practiced movement with little conscious effort. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/muscle%20memory))

## Examples

A former trumpet player says some of the learned physical skill remains.

> [[dude|Dude]], I played the trumpet in seventh grade and uh I still have a little bit of muscle memory from it.
