**sim** (/sɪm/) is an informal noun for a **simulation**, especially a game that imitates a real activity, such as a flight sim or dating sim.

**SIM** also means **subscriber identity module**, used to identify a subscriber to a mobile network; a **SIM card** contains one.

Source: [Cambridge — sim](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/sim).
