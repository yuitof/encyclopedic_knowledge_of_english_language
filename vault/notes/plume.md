**plume** (/pluːm/) is a noun for a rising cloud or column of smoke, dust, steam, or similar material. It can also mean a large feather or a decorative group of feathers.

Source: [OALD — plume](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/plume).

## Examples

Oxford describes material rising from a volcano:

> A great plume of dust and ash rose from the volcano.
