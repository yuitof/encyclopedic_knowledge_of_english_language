**Parthenon Frieze** is the band of low-relief marble sculpture made for the upper walls of the Parthenon’s inner structure in fifth-century BCE Athens.

It belongs to the classical visual-art background relevant to studies of poetry and [[ekphrasis]].

Source: [Wikipedia — Parthenon Frieze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parthenon_Frieze).
