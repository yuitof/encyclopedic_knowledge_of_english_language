**cake-up** (/ˈkeɪk ʌp/) is a mocking [[slang]] noun for [[makeup]] applied so thickly that it looks heavy or caked on. It plays on **[[makeup]]** and **cake**. The expression criticises the amount or appearance of the cosmetics.

Source: [Urban Dictionary — cake-up, Matthew Lake’s 2006 entries](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=cake-up).
