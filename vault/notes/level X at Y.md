**levelled** (/ˈlevld/) is the past [[participle]] of **level**, which in **level X at Y** means to direct blame, criticism, or an accusation publicly at someone.

**X** is what is directed, such as blame or criticism; **Y** is the person or group targeted. The article uses **have levelled**, in [[the present perfect tense]]. **Levelled** is the [[british|British]] spelling; American English normally uses **leveled**. **Against** can replace **at** in this pattern.

Sources: [OALD — level at](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/level-at); [Cambridge — level something against/at someone](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/level-against-at).

## Examples

The article describes politicians from opposing parties blaming Macron for the disorder. Article excerpt:

> levelled the blame for the disorder at Macron

Here, **X = the blame for the disorder** and **Y = Macron**.

Later, the article repeats the pattern when describing presidential contenders blaming Macron and his ministers: **X = the blame** and **Y = Macron and his ministers**. This second occurrence is paraphrased here.
