**level something at someone** (/ˈlevl ˌsʌmθɪŋ ət ˈsʌmwʌn/) means to direct blame, criticism, or accusations at them.

Sources: [OALD — level at](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/level-at); [Cambridge — level something against/at someone](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/level-against-at).

## Examples

Politicians blame Macron for the unrest. Article excerpt:

> Leading figures from both parties have levelled the blame for the disorder at Macron […]

— [BBC News — France protests report, Jaroslav Lukiv](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c95ynd587ynzo).
