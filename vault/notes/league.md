league
- **in a league of someone's own**
- **be in a different league**
: to be much better than something or someone else

- They're in a different league from us.
- When it comes to cooking, I'm not in her league
- A house like that is out of our league
(OALD)
- Our last hotel was good but this was in a different league.
(Cambridge Dictionary)
