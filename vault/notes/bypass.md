**bypass** (/ˈbaɪpɑːs/) is a transitive verb with two main senses:

1. To go around or avoid a place or obstacle. For example, a new road can bypass a town.
2. To ignore a rule, official process, or person in authority, especially to get something done more quickly. For example, someone may bypass the usual approval procedure.

Source: [Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary — bypass](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/bypass_2).
