**miss out** (/ˌmɪs ˈaʊt/) is a phrasal verb meaning to lose an opportunity to take part in or enjoy something useful or fun. The common pattern is **miss out on + noun/gerund**. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/miss-out))
