**sleepover** (/ˈsliːpəʊvə/) is an overnight stay at someone else's home.

- **sleepover** is a noun about where you spend the night.
- **[[oversleep]]** is a verb meaning to sleep past the time you intended to wake up.

## Examples

Context: The hosts invite a guest to spend the night with them.

> This is the perfect place for a sleepover.
