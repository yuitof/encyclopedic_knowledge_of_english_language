Dos, Don'ts
: Do, Don't に s つけたもの


_Dos and don'ts_ is an especially unusual exception. The apostrophe in the contraction _doesn't_ seems to make people want to use an apostrophe to make _do_ plural (_do's and don'ts_), but then to be consistent, you'd also have to use an apostrophe to make _don't_ plural, which becomes [[downright]] ugly (_do's and don't's_).
