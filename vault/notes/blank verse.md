**blank verse** (/ˌblæŋk ˈvɜːs/) is unrhymed metrical poetry, especially [[iambic]] pentameter in English.

Milton’s [[Paradise Lost]] is an example. **Blank** refers to the absence of [[rhyme]], rather than the absence of metre. Related: [[iambic]].

Sources: [Merriam-Webster — blank verse](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/blank%20verse); [Wikipedia — Blank verse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blank_verse).
