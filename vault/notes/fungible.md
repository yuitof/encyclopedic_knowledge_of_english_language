**fungible** (/ˈfʌndʒəbəl/) is an adjective meaning readily adaptable to new situations.

## Examples

Context: Jensen Huang describes Nvidia's computing architecture as usable across many kinds of AI models and links that adaptability to low cost.

> [[on|On]] [[the]] other hand, because we're fungible, we're also [[the]] lowest cost.
