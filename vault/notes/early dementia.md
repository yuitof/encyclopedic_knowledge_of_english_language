**early dementia** (/ˌɜːli dɪˈmenʃə/) is [[dementia]] in its first stage, when changes in memory, thinking, or language begin to affect daily life. Symptoms are often mild, and many people can still manage most daily activities independently. [Alzheimer’s Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/early-stages-dementia)

**Early-onset dementia** refers to dementia that begins before age 65. [Alzheimer Society of Canada](https://www.alzheimer.ca/en/about-dementia/other-types-dementia/young-onset-dementia/what-is)
