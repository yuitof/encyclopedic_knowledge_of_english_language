**sleep deprivation** (/ˈsliːp deprɪˌveɪʃən/) is the condition of not getting enough sleep.

Source: [Cambridge — sleep deprivation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/sleep-deprivation).

## Examples

At an immersive light-and-mirror attraction, Luke wonders whether his lack of sleep explains his reaction.
