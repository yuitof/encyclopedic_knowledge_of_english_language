**redacted** (/rɪˈdæktɪd/) is used to describe a document with information removed or obscured.

- **redact**: hide specific information in a document, often for privacy or confidentiality.
- **censor**: restrict communication because of its content.

## Examples

Context: Discussing information withheld from the released Epstein files.

> … after everything we now know, 70 to 80% of the files are still redacted …

[The Epstein Files. — Volksgeist, 56:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SOW-7mCsutg&t=3392s)
