**excursion** (/ɪkˈskɜːrʒən/) is a trip or outing, usually a short one taken for pleasure.

## Examples

Context: A speaker jokes [[about]] searching for his father during a trip.

> We going to go try to find him [[on]] [[the]] excursion.
