**omnibuses** (/ˈɒmnɪbəsɪz/) is the plural of **omnibus**, an older word for a public bus.

## Examples

Clarissa observes the London traffic in [[Mrs Dalloway]].

> She stood for a moment, looking at the omnibuses in [[Piccadilly]].
