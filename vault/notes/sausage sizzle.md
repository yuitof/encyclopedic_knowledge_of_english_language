**sausage sizzle** (/ˈsɒsɪdʒ ˌsɪzəl/) is an Australian and New Zealand term for a barbecue at which sausages are cooked and eaten. ([Collins English Dictionary](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/sausage-sizzle))

Countable noun. Community sausage sizzles often raise funds by selling sausages in bread.

![Volunteers serving a sausage sizzle outside Bunnings](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/db/Bunnings_Sausage_sizzle.jpg)

Photo: Henk Tobbe, [Bunnings Sausage sizzle](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bunnings_Sausage_sizzle.jpg), CC0. The pictured Rotary Club event raised money for charities.

## Examples

Max Fosh and I Did A Thing use a homemade jet engine to cook sausages, then serve them to people nearby. Published coverage reports that they call the event “the world’s first jet engine sausage sizzle.” They also build [[Wolverine claws]] to hold the sausages.
