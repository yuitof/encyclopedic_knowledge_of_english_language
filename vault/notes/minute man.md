**minute man** (/ˈmɪnɪt mæn/) is derogatory sexual slang for a man who reaches orgasm undesirably quickly. It should not be confused with the one-word historical term **minuteman** (/ˈmɪnɪtmən/), a colonial militia member prepared to fight at a minute's notice.

## Examples

In a jokingly suggestive conversation, a speaker says:

> "I'm normally a minute man."
