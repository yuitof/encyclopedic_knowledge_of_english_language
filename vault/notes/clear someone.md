**clear someone** (/klɪə ˈsʌmwʌn/) is a transitive verb pattern meaning to prove or declare that someone is innocent, not responsible, or no longer under suspicion. It commonly appears as **clear someone of something**.

## Examples

After an investigation in a comedic Star Wars story, Palpatine says:

> "And so, they finally cleared me from the Sith list."
