**off stream** (/ɔːf striːm/) is an expression meaning when a live internet broadcast is not taking place. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/stream))

## Examples

Two people clarify whether a previous remark was made during a broadcast.

> On stream or off stream?
