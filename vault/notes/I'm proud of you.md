**I'm proud of you** (/aɪm ˈpraʊd əv juː/) is a warm way to tell someone that you feel pleased and [[impressed]] by what they've done or by the kind of person they are. ([Oxford Advanced American Dictionary, “proud”](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/american_english/proud_1))

A parent might say it when their child [[put in the effort|puts in the effort]] to learn something difficult, makes progress, or does something kind. To name what prompted the praise, use **I'm proud of you for** followed by an **-ing** form.

## Examples

In the documentary *In a Different Key*, Caren Zucker tells her son Mickey she is proud of him for making it through his graduation ceremony even though the crowd overwhelmed him.

> I'm proud of you.
