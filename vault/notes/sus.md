**sus** (/sʌs/) is informal slang for **suspicious** or **suspect**; it describes a person or thing that may not be trustworthy. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/wordplay/what-does-sus-mean))

## Examples

Context: In an AI-generated Star Wars parody, a character responds after another character suspects that Anakin is lying.

> This dude isn't sus at all.

[Star Wars RUINED by AI - Part II — Tjabian, 12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L0LUeNkzj68&t=776s)

Here, **sus** means “suspicious”; the speaker says the character does not seem suspicious.
