**ad hominem** (/ˌæd ˈhɒmɪnem/) is a formal adjective describing criticism directed at a person’s character rather than their argument.

## In debate

An ad hominem fallacy uses an irrelevant personal criticism as a reason to reject an opponent’s claim. It distracts from the evidence and does not [[refute]] the argument. An insult alone is not necessarily an argument, and relevant questions about a source’s credibility are not automatically fallacious.

## Examples

Merriam-Webster quotes Lev Nachman’s warning about political language:

> But politicians need to avoid ad hominem attacks, accusations of treason, and demonizing swaths of society.

— Lev Nachman, *Foreign Affairs*, 1 August 2025, quoted in [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/ad%20hominem).
