- POSCA is an ideal tool to decorate your windows and display cases. All you need is [[ragged|a damp rag]] to clean your creations. Be careful however, if there’s a scratch on a very old pane that has become **porous** on its surface, the pigments can become encrusted. They will no longer be erasable.
