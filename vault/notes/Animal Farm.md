**Animal Farm** is George Orwell’s 1945 political [[fable]] about farm animals who overthrow their owner, only to come under the pigs’ domination. Its allegory draws on the Russian Revolution and Stalinism.

Source: [Wikipedia — Animal Farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Farm).
