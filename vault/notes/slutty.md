**slutty** (/ˈslʌti/) is an informal, disapproving adjective describing someone’s appearance or behaviour as sexually provocative or associated with many sexual partners. It can be insulting and [[judgemental]]; it is not a [[neutral]] description.

Sources: [Cambridge — sluttish, also slutty](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/sluttish); [Dictionary.com — slutty](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/slutty).
