**incongruity** (/ˌɪnkənˈɡruːəti/) is the strangeness you notice when something feels out of place or when two things fit together oddly. You can say **the incongruity of** a detail or **the incongruity between** two things. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/incongruity) · [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/incongruity)

An **incongruity** draws attention to the odd fit, as when a cheerful soundtrack plays during a solemn scene. A [[discrepancy]] draws attention to a difference between details that should agree.
