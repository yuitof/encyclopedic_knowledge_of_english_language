**seducing** (/sɪˈdjuːsɪŋ/, US /sɪˈduːsɪŋ/) is the present [[participle]] or gerund form of **seduce**, a transitive verb meaning to persuade someone to [[have sex]] with you through attraction. It can also mean persuading someone to do something by making it seem very attractive.

Source: [OALD — seduce, meanings and verb forms](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/seduce).
