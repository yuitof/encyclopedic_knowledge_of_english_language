**unhinged** (/ʌnˈhɪndʒd/) is an adjective meaning extremely disturbed, unstable, or irrational. In casual online speech, it is often used as an exaggeration for [[wild]] or outrageous behaviour.

Sources: [Merriam-Webster — unhinged](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/unhinged); [Cambridge — pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/unhinged).

## Examples

Mark Rober jokes that Michael Reeves’s planned cheating device would be too outrageous for his channel:

> I don't know what you were planning, but I know it's way too unhinged for my channel.

— [Last Cheater Standing Wins $10,000 — Mark Rober, around 1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_L1Rim7-IPo&t=98s).
