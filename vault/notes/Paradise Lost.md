**Paradise Lost** is John Milton’s epic poem in [[blank verse]], first published in 1667. It tells of Satan’s rebellion, the temptation of Adam and Eve, and their expulsion from Eden.

The first edition had ten books; the revised 1674 edition had twelve. Its metre is [[iambic]] pentameter.
