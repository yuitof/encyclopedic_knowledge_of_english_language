**shocked** (/ʃɑːkt/) is the past [[tense]] of **shock**, meaning to surprise or upset someone greatly. **To my core** emphasizes how deeply the feeling affected the speaker. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/shock))

## Examples

Nimi reacts to unexpected [[fan]] art of Smeagol.

> Shocked me to my core. It was It was certain [[fan]] art of Smeagol.

[42 Minutes Of Nimi Yapping (Compilation 58) 【Nimi Nightmare】 — around 0:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9eqj4ASnv9Q&t=8s)

The expression also occurs with the past-tense verb **struck** and the possessive form **his core**.

After searching Obi-Wan's hut, Vader finds something that affects him deeply.

> When he searched through the hut and found something that struck him to his core in the hut was some sort of chest.

[What if Darth Vader Found Yoda?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DPC-7kx6fs&t=166) — Vader's Order, 2:46; watched 18 November 2026.

Later in the same video, the narrator describes another discovery that changes Vader's reaction.

> There wasn't much that terrified him. But this struck him to his core, so he ignited his saber and quickly rushed outside the cave.

[What if Darth Vader Found Yoda?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DPC-7kx6fs&t=317) — Vader's Order, 5:17.
