**shocked** (/ʃɑːkt/) is the past [[tense]] of **shock**, meaning to surprise or upset someone greatly. **To my core** emphasizes how deeply the feeling affected the speaker. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/shock))
