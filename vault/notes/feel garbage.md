**feel like garbage** (/fiːl laɪk ˈɡɑːbɪdʒ/) is an informal expression meaning to feel very unwell or generally awful. The usual wording includes **like**. The form found in the video is **feeling like garbage**.

After discussing naps that leave them [[groggy]], a speaker says that long naps after streaming make them wake up feeling like garbage.
