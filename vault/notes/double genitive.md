**double genitive** (/ˌdʌbl ˈdʒenɪtɪv/) is a grammatical construction that combines **of** with a possessive noun or pronoun.

It explains the pattern **a friend of his**. **A [[fan]] of his** follows the same pattern. **Him** is an object pronoun, so **a [[fan]] of him** has a different construction.

Sources: [Apostrophe Protection Society — Double Genitive](https://www.apostrophe.org.uk/apostrophe-use); [Chicago Manual of Style — double possessive](https://www.chicagomanualofstyle.org/qanda/data/faq/topics/Usage/faq0131.html).
