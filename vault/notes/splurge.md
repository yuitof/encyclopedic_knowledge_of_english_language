**splurge** (/splɜːdʒ/) is an informal transitive or intransitive verb meaning to spend a lot of money on something unnecessary or indulgent. Common patterns are **splurge on something** and **splurge something on something**.

Unlike [[squander]], **splurge** can describe a deliberate treat or luxury without necessarily suggesting that the spending was careless or foolish.
