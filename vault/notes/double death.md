**double death** is informal gaming slang for a situation in which the player and an opponent, especially a boss, die in the same exchange or almost simultaneously. It overlaps with **double KO** in fighting games.

## Examples

After both the player and opponent die at nearly the same time, the streamer says:

> "Oh yeah, high five double death my dude."
