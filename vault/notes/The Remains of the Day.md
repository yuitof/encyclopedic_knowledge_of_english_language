**The Remains of the Day** is Kazuo Ishiguro’s 1989 novel, narrated by Stevens, an English [[butler]] [[looking back]] on his service at Darlington Hall and his relationship with Miss Kenton. It won the 1989 Booker Prize.

Related: [[Never Let Me Go]], [[The Buried Giant]].
