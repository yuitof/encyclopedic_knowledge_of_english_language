**Thanksgiving** (/ˌθæŋksˈɡɪvɪŋ/) is a national holiday in the United States, celebrated on the fourth Thursday in November. ([U.S. National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/features/thanksgiving))

**Happy Thanksgiving!** is a greeting you can use to wish someone a good holiday.

In 2026, Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26.
