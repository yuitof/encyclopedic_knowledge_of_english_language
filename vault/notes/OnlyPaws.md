**OnlyPaws** (/ˈəʊnli pɔːz/) is a humorous parody of [[OnlyFans]], usually applied to pet content such as pictures of animals' paws, often called “toe beans.” It is a pun rather than a single official service.

[[OnlyFans]] is a subscription-based platform where creators make content available to paying fans; adult material is strongly associated with it, though the platform is not limited to that. **OnlyPaws** borrows the name but shifts the focus from fans to animals' paws.
