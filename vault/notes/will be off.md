will be off
: [[will be off]] で「出発する」とかそういうのにもなりそうで、この off の用法は set off などのフレーズにも見られる

- He collected his tools and personal belongings, and **set off** in search of a new occupation.

: [[be off to X]] で X という目的地を示すこともできる

- If she worried about these parties he would not let her give them. Did she wish she had married Peter? But he must go. He *must be off,* he said, getting up.

Woolf, Virginia. Mrs Dalloway (Oxford World's Classics) (p. 177). (Function). Kindle Edition.
