**just around the corner** (/dʒʌst əˈraʊnd ðə ˈkɔːrnər/) is an expression meaning very nearby. ([Collins](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/around-the-corner-round-the-corner))

## Examples

A character suggests a nearby Indian restaurant.

> Hi, there's a decent Indian just around the corner.
