**you do you** (/juː duː juː/) is an informal way to say someone can do whatever they want or choose what suits them. Depending on tone, it can sound encouraging or dismissive, like **suit yourself**. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/you-do-you) · [Yale Grammatical Diversity Project](https://ygdp.yale.edu/phenomena/you-do-you)

**You can do you** (/juː kən duː juː/) makes the freedom to choose more explicit.

## Examples

In a conversation about karaoke, one speaker lightly teases the other for rarely singing, then leaves the choice up to him.

> Hey, you do you, my man.
