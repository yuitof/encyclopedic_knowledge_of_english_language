**Piccadilly** is a major street in central London, running between Hyde Park Corner and Piccadilly Circus.

Literary context: Clarissa walks near it, and its traffic forms part of the London setting of [[Mrs Dalloway]].

Source: [Wikipedia — Piccadilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piccadilly).
