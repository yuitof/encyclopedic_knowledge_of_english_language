**tripping** (/ˈtrɪpɪŋ/) is mainly US slang for thinking or acting in a way that seems unreasonable. **Are you tripping?** expresses disbelief at what someone said or did, much like **Are you serious?** It can sound teasing or confrontational. [Cambridge Dictionary, “be tripping”](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/be-tripping)

**Am I tripping?** can mean **Did I imagine that?** when you're unsure what you heard. If someone’s voice keeps dropping on a call, you can say **You’re [[cutting in and out]]**.

## Examples

During a VR hide-and-seek game, a player thinks they heard a sound and asks whether another player heard it too.

> Did you hear it, too? Or was it just me? Am I tripping?
>
> I thought I heard something.
