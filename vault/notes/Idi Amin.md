**Idi Amin** (/ˌiːdi ˌɑːˈmiːn/) was a Ugandan military dictator who ruled Uganda from 1971 to 1979. He seized power in a [[coup]] against Milton Obote. His rule was marked by mass killings and the expulsion of Uganda’s Asian minority. Ugandan rebels and Tanzanian forces overthrew him in 1979.

Sources: [Library of Congress — Idi Amin’s coup and rule](https://blogs.loc.gov/law/2018/01/today-in-history-idi-amin-overthrows-president-milton-obote-in-uganda/); [Cambridge — Idi Amin pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/idi-amin).
