**ghetto** (/ˈɡetəʊ/, US /ˈɡetoʊ/) is an often disapproving term for a poor area of a city where a particular group lives isolated from the rest of the population. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/ghetto))

Crime is commonly associated with the word, but “ghetto” does not mean “criminals.” Using it to imply criminality relies on a [[stereotype]] and may be offensive. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/ghetto))
