to death
: **starve to death** (/stɑrv tə dɛθ/) is an intransitive verb phrase meaning to die from lack of food. Source: [Merriam-Webster — starve, intransitive sense 1a](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/starve).

**fight to [[the]] death** (/faɪt tə ðə dɛθ/) is a verb phrase meaning to keep fighting until someone dies. Source: [Merriam-Webster — to the death, sense 1](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/to%20the%20death).

**shocked me to my core** (/ʃɑkt mi tə maɪ kɔr/) is a past-[[tense]] verb phrase meaning surprised or disturbed me very deeply. Sources: [Merriam-Webster — shock, verb sense 1a](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/shock), [core, noun sense 2c](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/core).
