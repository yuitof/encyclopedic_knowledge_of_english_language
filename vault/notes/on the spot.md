**on the spot** (/ɑːn ðə spɑːt/) is part of the expression **put someone on the spot**, meaning to place someone in a difficult position by demanding an immediate answer. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/on%20the%20spot))

## Examples

The host asks Angel an unexpected question about her relationship.

> I'm putting you on the spot.
