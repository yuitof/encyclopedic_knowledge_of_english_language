**scratch what I said** (/skrætʃ wʌt aɪ sɛd/) is an informal way to take back a [[statement]] you just made, often before correcting it. The shorter form **scratch that** works when it's clear which statement you mean. ([Cambridge Dictionary, “scratch that”](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/scratch?topic=cancelling-and-interrupting#:~:text=scratch%20that))

## Examples

During a lecture on local public goods, Jonathan Gruber takes back an explanation after mixing up the notation for towns and their populations.

> That's backwards. Scratch what I said. There are towns denoted by i.
