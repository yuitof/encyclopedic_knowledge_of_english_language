**Mulan** is Disney’s 1998 animated musical film inspired by the Chinese legend of Hua Mulan. A young woman disguises herself as a man and [[join|joins]] the army in her father’s place. Themes include family duty, courage, and identity.

Disney also released a live-action adaptation in 2020.

Sources: [Disney — Mulan (1998)](https://movies.disney.com/mulan); [Disney — Mulan (2020)](https://movies.disney.com/mulan-2020).
