**gold digger** (/ˈɡəʊld dɪɡə(r)/, US /ˈɡoʊld dɪɡər/) is a disapproving term for a person who enters a romantic relationship in order to get money from the other person. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/gold-digger))

## Examples

Context: In a Minecraft [[prank]], the players joke that a character wants wood rather than diamonds.

> Cuz right now she's a gold digger for wood. She don't want diamonds, but she want wood.
