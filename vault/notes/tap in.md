**tap in** (/ˌtæp ˈɪn/) is informal slang meaning to join an activity or take another person's place, especially in a game, contest, or group effort. It is related to **tag in**.

## Examples

Discussing the rules of a game, one participant suggests another person could take over:

> "In this game though, somebody could tap in for the person going."
