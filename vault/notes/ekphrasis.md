**ekphrasis** (/ˈekfrəsɪs/) is a literary description of, or response to, a visual artwork.

A traditional example is Homer’s description of Achilles’ shield in the *Iliad*. Compare [[ut pictura poesis]], the broader comparison between poetry and painting.

Sources: [Merriam-Webster — ekphrasis](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/ekphrasis); [Wikipedia — Ekphrasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekphrasis).
