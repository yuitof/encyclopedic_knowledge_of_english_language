**deviate** (/ˈdiːvieɪt/) is a verb meaning to depart from an expected course, standard, or subject.

Common pattern: **deviate from something**. The verb ends in /eɪt/.

Source: [Merriam-Webster — deviate](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/deviate).
