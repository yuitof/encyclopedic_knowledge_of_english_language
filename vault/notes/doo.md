**doo** (/duː/) is chiefly used in the reduplicative informal noun **doo-doo**, a childlike term for feces. By extension, **be in deep doo-doo** means to be in serious trouble.

## Examples

Facing an immediate threat in a comedic Star Wars scene, Jar Jar says:

> "Mesa in big doo-doo this time."
