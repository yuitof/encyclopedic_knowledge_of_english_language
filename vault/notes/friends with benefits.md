**friends with benefits** (/ˌfrendz wɪð ˈben.ə.fɪts/) is an informal phrase for friends who have sex with each other without being in a committed romantic relationship. One person in that arrangement is **a friend with benefits**. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/friend-with-benefits)

**FWB** is short for **friends with benefits**. Say it as three letters, **F–W–B**. You may also see it written as **fwb**. [Dictionary.com](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/friend-with-benefits)
