giraffe
: キリン

**giraffe** (/dʒəˈrɑːf/) is a tall African animal with a very long neck, long legs, and a coat patterned with dark patches. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/giraffe))

Countable noun; plural **giraffes**.
