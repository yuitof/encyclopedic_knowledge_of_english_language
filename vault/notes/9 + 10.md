**9 + 10** (/naɪn plʌs ten/), read as *nine plus ten*, is a reference to a [[vine|Vine]] meme in which a boy answers the question “What's nine plus ten?” with “21.” The correct answer is 19; the incorrect answer is the joke.

Also known as **9 + 10 = 21**, **the 21 meme**, or **the 21 kid**. People repeat the question, deliberately answer *21*, or reuse the clip's audio to reference the original joke. It is a meme reference rather than a conventional idiom.

The original clip was posted to Vine in June 2013 and spread through reposts, remixes, and parodies. It had another wave of popularity in 2019. ([Know Your Meme: 9 + 10 = 21](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/9-10-21))
