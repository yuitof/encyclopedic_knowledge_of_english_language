**Frankenstein** is Mary Shelley’s 1818 novel, subtitled *The Modern Prometheus*. It follows Victor Frankenstein’s creation of a living being and the consequences of his rejection of it.

**Frankenstein** is the creator’s surname; the creature is not given that name in the novel. Related: [[Paradise Lost]].
