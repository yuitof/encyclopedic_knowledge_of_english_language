**starve to death** (/stɑːrv tə deθ/) is a phrase meaning to die because of a lack of food. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/starve))

## Examples

While discussing fictional vampires, the speaker imagines them exhausting their food supply.

> And then they would all starve to death.
