**red roach** (/red rəʊtʃ/) is an informal description of a reddish-brown cockroach. **Roach** is short for **cockroach**. The American cockroach (*Periplaneta americana*) is one example: it is reddish brown with a pale yellowish band behind its head. Colour alone does not identify the species.

Source: [University of Florida IFAS — American Cockroach](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/IN298).
