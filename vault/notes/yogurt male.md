**yogurt male** (/ˈjoʊ.ɡɚt meɪl/) is a joking internet label that parodies categories like [[alpha|alpha male]] and [[sigma|sigma male]]. You can use it sarcastically to poke fun at ranking men into types, especially by pretending that a yogurt male is even rarer or more impressive than a sigma male. Also spelled **yoghurt male**.

American pronunciation from Cambridge. [yogurt](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/yogurt) · [male](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/male)

## Examples

A Reddit commenter mimics the title of a video that lists signs you're a sigma male, then exaggerates how rare the made-up type is.

> 15 signs you’re a yogurt male (10x rarer than sigma!)
