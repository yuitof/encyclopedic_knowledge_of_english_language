**at someone's feet** (/ət ˈsʌmwʌnz fiːt/) is an expression that can figuratively suggest devotion, admiration, or submission to someone.

## Examples

[[Peter Walsh]] recalls Sally’s resistance to Hugh Whitbread’s social prestige in [[Mrs Dalloway]].

> She saw through Hugh Whitbread anyhow--the admirable Hugh--when Clarissa and the rest were at his feet.
