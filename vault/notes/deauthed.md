**deauthed** is an informal technical shortening of **deauthenticated**, the past [[tense]] or past [[participle]] of **deauthenticate**. In Wi-Fi contexts, it means that a device’s authenticated connection has been ended, disconnecting it from the wireless network.

Deauthentication can occur during normal network operation or be forced; the word alone does not establish why it happened.

Source: [Cisco Meraki — Common Wireless Event Log Messages and Issues](https://documentation.meraki.com/Wireless/Troubleshooting_and_Support/Troubleshooting/Common_Wireless_Event_Log_Messages_and_Issues).
