**infiltration** (/ˌɪnfɪlˈtreɪʃn/) is a noun meaning the act of secretly entering a place or organisation, especially to gather information or influence it. The verb is [[infiltrate]].

Source: [OALD — infiltration](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/infiltration).
