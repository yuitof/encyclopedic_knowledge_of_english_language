**buckle up** (/ˌbʌkl ˈʌp/) is a phrasal verb meaning to fasten your seat belt. As an instruction, it is also used figuratively to tell someone to prepare for an exciting, difficult, or unpredictable experience.

Sources: [Cambridge — buckle up](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/buckle-up); [Dictionary.com — buckle up, including figurative usage](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/buckle-up).

## Examples

Cambridge gives this instruction about driving:

> Don't forget, buckle up before you start driving.
