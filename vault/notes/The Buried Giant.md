**The Buried Giant** is Kazuo Ishiguro’s 2015 fantasy novel about the elderly couple Axl and Beatrice travelling through a post-Arthurian Britain affected by collective forgetting.

Its concerns include memory, love, and the relationship between forgetting and peace. Related: [[Never Let Me Go]], [[The Remains of the Day]].
