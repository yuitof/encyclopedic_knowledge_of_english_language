**I will do what I must** (/aɪ wɪl duː wɒt aɪ mʌst/) is a declaration of determined resolve: the speaker intends to carry out what they consider necessary, even when the action is difficult or unwelcome. Here, **will** expresses determination, while **must** expresses necessity.

## Examples

During a comedic retelling of the Obi-Wan and Anakin confrontation, a character says:

> "I will do what I must."

[Qui Gon reacts to \*Obi vs Ani\* for the First Time](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPNrYffKdco&t=321) — Charlie Hopkinson, 5:21; watched 20 November 2026.
