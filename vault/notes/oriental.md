oriental
: 東洋の

opp: [[occidental]]

```
The adjective oriental, which carries strong associations with Western colonialism and with language that treats people as exotic or "Other," is usually considered offensive when used by non-Asian people to describe people of various Asian identities. The same is true of the related noun. The adjective as used to describe elements of Asian culture often has a dated feel, and is increasingly regarded as offensive.
```

```
The word _oriental_ derives from the Latin _orient-_, _oriens_, meaning "the east" or "the part of the sky where the sun rises." _Occidental_, in contrast, derives from the Latin _occident-_, _occidens_, meaning "the west" or "the part of the sky where the sun sets." The geographical regions known as "the Orient" and "the Occident" are named for their positions relative to one another—east and west, respectively.
```
