**pee myself** (/piː maɪˈself/) is an informal expression meaning to urinate without being able to contain it. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/pee))

## Examples

The speaker recalls trying to hold in urine during a long ride.

> I thought I was going to pee myself like the whole time.
