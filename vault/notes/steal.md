**steal** (/stiːl/) is an informal noun for something bought at an unusually low price. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/steal))

## Examples

The shopper considers three items for 700 yen an excellent bargain.

> Having three for 700 yen is such a steal.
