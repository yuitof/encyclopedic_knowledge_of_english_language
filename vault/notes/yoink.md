**yoink** (/jɔɪŋk/) is a playful slang verb meaning to grab or take something quickly, often without permission. As an exclamation, **Yoink!** is something you say as you snatch something or pretend to. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/yoink)

## Examples

A VR player admires an avatar skin and says **yoink** as if quickly claiming it.

> Bro, this skin is awesome. Yoink!

[VR Scavenger Hunt 2, 4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N_Q45pve0_E&t=276s)

In a sponsored segment, the speaker uses **yoink** to mean steal data.

> Surfshark is a VPN, or virtual private network, that secures your data, so hackers can't yoink it, when you're using some sketchy public Wi-Fi.

[I Found The Baby Born Next To Me...30 Years Later, 5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9XrGxYMu9P8&t=348s)
