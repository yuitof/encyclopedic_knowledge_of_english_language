**more of** (/mɔːr əv/) is a phrase used to say that one description fits a person or thing better than another. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/more%20of))

## Examples

A presenter describes the [[kind of]] video game he is pitching.

> So this one is it's more of a psychological game.
