**slip** (/slɪp/) is in historical men’s formal dress, an under-waistcoat whose edge shows beneath the outer waistcoat.

This is the relevant sense for the men’s **white slips** in [[Mrs Dalloway]]. It differs from the familiar women’s undergarment also [[called]] a slip.

Sources: [Wikipedia — Slip (clothing)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slip_(clothing)); [Virginia Woolf, *Mrs. Dalloway* — Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/71865/pg71865.txt).
