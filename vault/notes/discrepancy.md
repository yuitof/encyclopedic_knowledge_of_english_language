**discrepancy** (/dɪˈskrepənsi/) is a difference between things you expect to agree, such as two figures for the same amount or two accounts of the same event. The usual pattern is **a discrepancy between A and B**. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/discrepancy)

A **discrepancy** draws attention to where the details differ, as when two records show different totals. An [[incongruity]] draws attention to how oddly something fits its setting.
