**hump** (/ˈhʌmp/) is an often vulgar transitive verb meaning to [[have sex]] with someone. It can also describe an animal rubbing its genitals against another animal or an object, but that is a separate usage.

Sources: [Merriam-Webster — hump](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/hump), [Collins English Dictionary — hump](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/hump).
