**grassroots movement** (/ˈɡrɑːsruːts ˈmuːvmənt/) is a social or political movement driven mainly by ordinary people at the community or local level rather than established leaders, parties, or institutions. It typically seeks to build support person to person and influence public opinion, policy, or collective action.

Sources: [Britannica — grassroots](https://www.britannica.com/topic/grassroots), [Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary — grassroots](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/grassroots).
