**Jew** (/dʒuː/) is one of the four playable character classes in *[[South Park]]: The Stick of Truth*, alongside **Fighter**, **Mage**, and **Thief**. The class name and abilities are part of the game’s [[religious]] satire. Its combat style includes status effects and abilities that become stronger as the character loses health.

Sources: [South Park Studios — Which Class Do You Choose?](https://southpark.cc.com/news/3ir4s6/which-class-do-you-choose); [South Park Archives — The New Kid, classes](https://southpark.wiki.gg/wiki/The_New_Kid).
