**as always** (/əz ˈɔːlweɪz/) is an adverbial phrase meaning in the usual or expected way, just as on previous occasions. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary: always, “as always”](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/always))

It can come before or after an expression of thanks. It presents the appreciation as something familiar and continuing, often acknowledging someone's repeated support. Separate it from the rest of the sentence with a comma.

User-provided example: *Thank you for watching, as always.* The phrase can also go first: *As always, thank you for watching.*

## Examples

At the end of a video about making a cosplay costume, the creator expresses continuing appreciation for the audience.

> As always, I am eternally grateful.

— [I spent 100+ HOURS to cosplay for BIGGEST Anime con in North America — 21:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G1IAriCSXnk&t=1287s)

In a video about spending a week in VR, LukeJ describes his usual team affiliation.

> I'm repping Team J, as always.

— [I Spent 7 Days In VR, Here's What It Does To Your Brain — 18:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=efHFzNrDG5Y&t=1089s)
