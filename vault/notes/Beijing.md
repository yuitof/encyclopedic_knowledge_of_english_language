**Beijing** (/ˌbeɪˈdʒɪŋ/) is the capital city of China. In political and diplomatic reporting, **Beijing** often refers to **China’s central government or leadership**.

This is **metonymy**: referring to an institution by a closely associated place. **Washington** can similarly refer to the US federal government. Context determines whether the city or the government is intended.

Sources: [Cambridge — Beijing](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/beijing?q=Beijing); [American Heritage Dictionary — metonymy](https://ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=metonymy); [Propaganda, Disinformation, and the Power of Words — capital-city metonymy](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5ee500d316a2470c370596d3/t/5ef382227c42d35ef1518ebd/1593016872235/Propaganda_Disinformation_and_the_Power_of_Words.pdf).
