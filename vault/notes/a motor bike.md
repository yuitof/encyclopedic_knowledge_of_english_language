**motorbike** (/ˈməʊtəbaɪk/), also written **motor bike**, is an informal term, especially in British English, for a motorcycle. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/motorbike))

## Comparison

**Motorbike** and **bicycle** remain distinct everyday categories: a motorbike is powered by an engine, while a bicycle is driven by pedals. Because **bike** commonly refers to a bicycle, the **motor** element still performs a useful disambiguating role.

By contrast, [[a motor car]] is now old-fashioned or formal because **car** alone normally means an automobile. [[carriage]] usually identifies the horse-drawn vehicle, so the **motor** prefix is no longer needed in everyday use.
