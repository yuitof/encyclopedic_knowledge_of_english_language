**skimp on** (/ˌskɪmp ˈɒn/) is a phrasal verb meaning to spend or use less time, money, effort, or material on something than is really needed, often at the cost of quality or completeness.

Near-synonyms: [cut corners](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/cut-corners), [be stingy with](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/stingy), [economize on](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/economize), and [save on](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/save_1). These overlap but are not interchangeable: **economize** and **save** are neutral, while **skimp** and **cut corners** usually imply an inadequate result.

## Examples

Explaining the phrase, the instructor says:

> "If someone says don't skimp on the ingredients, they mean that one shouldn't use less of the ingredients than the recipe calls for."
