**Jack Daniel’s** (/ˌdʒæk ˈdænjəlz/) is an American brand of Tennessee whiskey produced by the Jack Daniel Distillery in Lynchburg, Tennessee. The distillery has been officially registered since 1866 and is known for mellowing its whiskey through sugar-maple charcoal. ([Jack Daniel’s](https://www.jackdaniels.com/en-us/BHM/NearestandJack))
