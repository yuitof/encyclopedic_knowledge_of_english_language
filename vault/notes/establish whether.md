**establish whether** (/ɪˈstæblɪʃ ˈweðə/) is a verb phrase meaning to determine or find out if something is true, possible, or the case. It is followed by a clause, as in **establish whether + subject + verb**.

Sources: [Cambridge Dictionary — establish](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/establish), [Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary — if / whether](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/if_1).
