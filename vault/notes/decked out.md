**decked out** (/ˌdekt ˈaʊt/) is the past [[participle]] of **deck out**, meaning dressed or decorated elaborately.

In [[Mrs Dalloway]], the description of the world as decked out is figurative: dawn makes it visible and colourful again.

Sources: [Merriam-Webster — deck](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/deck); [Virginia Woolf, *Mrs. Dalloway* — Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/71865/pg71865.txt).
