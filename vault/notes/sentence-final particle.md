**sentence-final particle** (/ˌsentəns ˈfaɪnl ˈpɑːtɪkl/) is a word placed at the end of an utterance to convey the speaker’s attitude or manage the interaction.

In conversational English, certain final words and tags can serve similar discourse functions. Their grammatical classification varies; a tag question is not automatically a [[particle]].

## Examples

The source lists this English tag question as a comparable [[sentence]]-final expression.

> They're not hurt, are they?
