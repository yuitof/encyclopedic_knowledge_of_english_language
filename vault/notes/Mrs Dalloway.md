**Mrs Dalloway** is Virginia Woolf’s 1925 novel about a day in London as Clarissa Dalloway prepares for a [[party]]. Its shifting viewpoints connect present experience with memories, especially through Clarissa, [[Peter Walsh]], and the war veteran Septimus Warren Smith.

The motor car and skywriting aeroplane gather strangers’ attention. Read as literary devices, they connect separate viewpoints while also raising questions about authority, public spectacle, and interpretation.

## Examples

The opening [[sentence]] establishes Clarissa’s immediate purpose.

> Mrs. Dalloway said she would buy the flowers herself.
