**Peter Walsh** is a character in Virginia Woolf’s [[Mrs Dalloway]]. A former suitor of Clarissa, he returns from India and remains preoccupied with their past relationship.

SparkNotes interprets his criticism of Clarissa and Richard as mixed with insecurity and unresolved attachment.
