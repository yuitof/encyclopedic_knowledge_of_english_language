**Arrowsmith** is Sinclair Lewis’s 1925 novel about Martin Arrowsmith, a doctor and researcher confronting pressures on scientific work. It was awarded the 1926 Pulitzer Prize, which Lewis declined.

Source: [Wikipedia — Arrowsmith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrowsmith_(novel)).
