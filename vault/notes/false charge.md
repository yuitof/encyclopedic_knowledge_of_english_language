**false charge** (/fɔːls tʃɑːdʒ/) is a noun phrase for an untrue accusation.

Here **charge** means an accusation, rather than a fee.

## Examples

Collins quotes the *Times, Sunday Times*.

> He said that he planned to sue her for making the 'false charge'.
