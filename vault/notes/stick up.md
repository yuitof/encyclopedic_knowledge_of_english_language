**stick up** (/ˌstɪk ˈʌp/) is a phrasal verb meaning to point upward or protrude above a surface. In the phrase **stick up for someone**, it means to defend or support that person.

## Examples

Navigating rocks in an inflatable boat, the narrator notices sharp objects protruding upward:

> "There were some rocks sticking up."

[RAINBOLT Challenged me to reach Impossible Coordinates...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-0pX5MfDBas&t=1079) — Magnus Midtbø, 17:59; watched 7–8 October 2026.

Responding to public criticism of fellow creators, a speaker says:

> "I appreciate those sticking up for them and I hope you know it means a lot to the three of us."
