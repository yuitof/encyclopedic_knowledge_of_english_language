**pay at the till** (/peɪ ət ðə tɪl/) is a British English phrase for paying for your items at a shop's checkout. The **till** is the cash register or the place where the [[cashier]] takes payment. In American English, **pay at the register** is common. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary, “till”](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/till_2)

## Examples

Co-op uses the phrase to explain how shoppers can pay in the store and have their groceries delivered later.

> Pay at the till and we’ll make sure your Co-op home delivery is on your doorstep soon afterwards.

[Co-op, “Shop and Deliver”](https://www.coop.co.uk/ways-to-shop)
