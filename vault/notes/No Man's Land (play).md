**No Man’s Land** is Harold Pinter’s play written in 1974 and first performed in 1975. Its four characters, Hirst, Spooner, Foster, and Briggs, inhabit an unstable meeting point of memory, performance, and imagined identities.

The title can be read figuratively beside [[no-man's-land]].

Sources: [Grove Atlantic — No Man’s Land](https://groveatlantic.com/book/no-mans-land/); [Nobel Prize — Pinter bibliography](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/pinter-bio-bibl-1.pdf).
