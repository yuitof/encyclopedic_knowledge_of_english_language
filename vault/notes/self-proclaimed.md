**self-proclaimed** (/ˌself prəˈkleɪmd/) is an adjective describing a title or quality claimed by the person themselves. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/self-proclaimed))

A Minecraft builder describes the escape-room expert invited to test his base.
