**escape gear** (/ɪˈskeɪp ɡɪə/) is an informal way to describe the **escape wheel** of a mechanical clock or watch. The escape wheel is the final toothed wheel in the gear train; it works with the escapement to release power in regular steps and control the timekeeping motion.

![[escapement-graham-diagram.png|Drawing of a Graham escapement, labeling the escape wheel, pallets, and pendulum crutch]]

The Graham escapement diagram labels **(a)** the escape wheel, **(b)** the pallets, and **(c)** the pendulum crutch.

![[escape-wheel-tower-clock.jpg|A verge escapement in a tower clock, showing its crown wheel and verge]]

The photograph shows a verge escapement from a tower clock, with the crown wheel visible below the horizontal verge.

The standard technical terms are **escape wheel** for the wheel and **escapement** for the mechanism that regulates it.
