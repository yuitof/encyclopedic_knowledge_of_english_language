**islander** (/ˈaɪləndə(r)/) is a person who lives on an island, especially a small one. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/islander))

## Examples

Context: The Philippines is an archipelago of more than 7,000 islands. ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/How-many-islands-make-up-the-Philippine-archipelago))

Example sentence:

> Many Filipinos are islanders because the country is an archipelago.
