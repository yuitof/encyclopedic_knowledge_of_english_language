open relationship
: An open relationship means having more than one romantic or sexual partner at a time. It’s an arrangement that both parties agree is non-exclusive or non-monogamous. As one or both partners engage in romantic or [sexual activities](https://www.webmd.com/sex-relationships/guide/health-sex-of-special-interest) outside the [relationship](https://www.webmd.com/sex-relationships/guide/relationships-marriage-and-health), the arrangement’s agreement aspect is key. 

If the two parties don’t agree to an open relationship or marriage and one or both still engage in multiple partnerships, it’s known as a nonconsensual, non-monogamous (NCNM) relationship.
