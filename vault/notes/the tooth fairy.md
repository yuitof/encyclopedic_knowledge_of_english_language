the tooth fairy
: 乳歯が抜けた時に the tooth fairy が来て、何か置いてくみたいな文化があるらしい

In medieval Europe, people were very careful with baby teeth. They believed witches could use them for magic, so they hid the teeth under pillows or burried them to keep children safe.
