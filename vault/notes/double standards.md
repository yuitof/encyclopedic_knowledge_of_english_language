**double standards** (/ˌdʌbəl ˈstændədz/) are unfair differences in the rules or expectations applied to people in comparable situations: one person or group is allowed to do something that another is criticized or punished for doing. Singular: **a double standard**. ([Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/double-standard))
