**hair follicle** (/ˈheə ˌfɒl.ɪ.kəl/) is the tube-like structure in the skin that surrounds the lower part of a hair shaft and supplies the growing root of the hair.

The example below uses the plural form **hair follicles**.

## Examples

While comparing human and chimpanzee hair density, the speaker says:

> "Patch for patch, your skin has roughly as many hair follicles as a chimp's."
