**social (verb)** (/ˈsəʊʃəl/) is not a standard dictionary verb. The usual verb is **socialize** (British also **socialise**, /ˈsəʊʃəlaɪz/), meaning to spend time with other people in a social way. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary — social](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/social_1); [Cambridge Dictionary — socialize](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/socialize))

Some casual or business contexts use **social** informally as a jargon verb, but its meaning depends on the surrounding context.
