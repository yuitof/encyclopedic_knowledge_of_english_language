**the worst thing you can do** (/ðə wɝːst θɪŋ ju kən duː/) is a way to single out the worst possible choice in a situation. Speakers often use it as a strong warning about what to avoid. **You** can refer to anyone in that situation. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary, “worst”](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/worst_1)

## Examples

An Elden Ring streamer says that getting overconfident is the biggest mistake a player can make.

> Being cocky in this game is, number one, the worst thing you can do.
