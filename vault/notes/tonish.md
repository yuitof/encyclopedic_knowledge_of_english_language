**tonish** (/ˈtɒnɪʃ/) is not a standard linguistic term. In language study, a language in which differences in pitch distinguish meaning is called **tonal** or a **tone language**; see [[tones]]. The rare English adjective **tonish**, also spelled **tonnish**, normally means stylish or fashionable rather than “having tones.”

Sources: [Collins English Dictionary — tonish](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/tonish), [Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary — tone language](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/tone-language).
