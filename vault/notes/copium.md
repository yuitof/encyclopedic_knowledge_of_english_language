**copium** (/ˈkəʊpiəm/) is an internet [[slang]] noun for an imaginary substance that supposedly helps someone remain unrealistically optimistic or deny a disappointing reality. It is often used mockingly. The word blends **cope** and **[[opium]]**.

Source: [Collins — copium](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/copium).
