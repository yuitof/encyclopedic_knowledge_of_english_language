**crack someone up** (/kræk ˈsʌmwʌn ʌp/) is an informal separable phrasal verb meaning to make someone laugh a lot. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary — crack up](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/crack-up))

The object can go between **crack** and **up**, as in **cracking me up**.

## Examples

Example sentence:

> This guy is cracking me up.

See also: [[crack up]].
