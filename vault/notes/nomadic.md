**nomadic** (/nəʊˈmædɪk/) is an adjective describing people or a way of life that involves moving from place to place rather than living permanently in one location. It can also describe someone who moves frequently for work or [[travel]].

Source: [Oxford — nomad and nomadic](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/american_english/nomad).
