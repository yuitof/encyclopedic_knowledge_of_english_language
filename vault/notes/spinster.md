**spinster** (/ˈspɪnstə/) is an old-fashioned and often offensive noun for an unmarried woman, especially one regarded as past the usual age for marriage. Historically, it also meant a woman whose occupation was spinning wool into thread.

The conventional male counterpart is **bachelor**, but the terms are not socially symmetrical: **bachelor** is generally less stigmatised today, whereas **spinster** is normally avoided unless discussing historical usage.
