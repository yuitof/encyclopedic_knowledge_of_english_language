**shallop** (/ˈʃæləp/) is a small open boat propelled by oars or sails, especially in shallow water.

## Examples

In [[Mrs Dalloway]], a boat carried on deep water becomes an image for Lady Bruton’s thoughts sinking into sleep.

> Borne like a frail shallop on deep, deep floods, Lady Bruton’s great-grandfather and his memoir and his [[campaign|campaigns]] in North America were whelmed and sunk.
