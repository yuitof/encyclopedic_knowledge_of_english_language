**sovereign** (/ˈsɒvrɪn/) is a formal noun meaning a king or queen.

## Examples

Men at a London club respond to the possibility that royalty is passing in [[Mrs Dalloway]].

> [[at once|At once]] they stood even straighter, and removed their hands, and seemed ready to attend their Sovereign, [[if need be]], to the cannon’s mouth, as their ancestors had done before them.
