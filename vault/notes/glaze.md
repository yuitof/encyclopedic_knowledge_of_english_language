**glazing** (/ˈɡleɪzɪŋ/) is the -ing form of **glaze**, a slang verb meaning to praise someone or something excessively. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/slang/glaze))

## Examples

Context: Crispy Concords [[compliment|compliments]] her word choice after she asks him for a funny “anecdote.” She responds that he is praising her excessively.

> **Her:** What's a random random anecdote[^anecdote] you have [[in the past]] 2 months that's funny to you?
>
> **Crispy:** What a good word.
>
> […]
>
> **Her:** You're glazing me.

[I Put Up A Billboard To Find Love — Crispy Concords, 21:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eRVogbCN7Zo&t=1312s)

[^anecdote]: **anecdote** (/ˈænɪkdoʊt/) is a short story about an interesting or amusing real incident, often something someone personally experienced. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/anecdote))
