**rubbing off on** (/ˌrʌbɪŋ ˈɒf ɒn/) is the -ing form of **rub off on**, meaning to pass a feeling, quality, or behaviour to someone through spending time together.

Source: [Cambridge — rub off on someone](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/rub-off-on).

## Examples

While discussing tiredness, one speaker describes being affected by the other’s energy.

> Your energy's rubbing off on me.
