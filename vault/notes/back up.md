back up
: ~~(転がったりしながら敵から)~~後ろに引くこと

**back up** (/ˌbæk ˈʌp/) is a phrasal verb meaning to move backwards, provide support, or make a spare copy of data. Compare [[back out]], which usually means to withdraw from an agreement or activity.

## Examples

In a game, one group tells another player to move backwards:

> "We're here to tell you you need to back up 10 steps from us right now."
