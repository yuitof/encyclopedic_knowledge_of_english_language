**congested** (/kənˈdʒestɪd/) describes a body part that is blocked with blood or mucus; it is the adjective form of **congest**. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/congested))

## Examples

Context: One of the players says that another player’s voice sounds blocked or stuffy.

> Bro, you sound congested.

[Renting a $1 vs $1,000 Girlfriend.. — NATER4L, 30:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ORUmv8nnoxM&t=1854s)

Here, **congested** describes a voice that sounds nasal or blocked, as it might during a cold or allergy.
