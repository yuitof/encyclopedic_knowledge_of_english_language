**crochet** (/kroʊˈʃeɪ/) is a verb meaning to make fabric or clothing by interlocking loops of yarn with a hooked needle. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/crochet))

## Examples

A participant offers to make another person a new shirt.

> Well, I could crochet you an entirely new shirt.
