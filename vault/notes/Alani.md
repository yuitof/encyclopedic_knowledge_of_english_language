**Alani** (/əˈlɑːni/) is the consumer-facing name used by **Alani Nu**, an American wellness company best known for brightly coloured, zero-sugar energy drinks.

![[alani-nu-cherry-slush-official.png|Alani Nu Cherry Slush energy-drink can]]

Image: Alani Nutrition, [Alani Nu Cherry Slush energy drink](https://www.alaninu.com/products/energy-drink-cherry-slush).
