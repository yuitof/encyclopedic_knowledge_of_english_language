**idyllic** (/ɪˈdɪlɪk/, also /aɪˈdɪlɪk/) is an adjective meaning peaceful, beautiful, and seemingly perfect, without problems. It often describes a place, holiday, or way of life.

Source: [OALD — idyllic](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/idyllic).

## Examples

Oxford uses it to describe an appealing place:

> The cottage sounds idyllic.
