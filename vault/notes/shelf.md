**shelf** (/ʃelf/) is a noun for a flat horizontal support used to hold things.

The plural is **shelves** (/ʃelvz/). In [[Mrs Dalloway]], Rezia’s image of a shelf appearing beneath her suggests a temporary feeling of support.

## Examples

The shelf is figurative: Rezia’s momentary emotional support gives way.

> Turning, the shelf fell; down, down she dropped.
