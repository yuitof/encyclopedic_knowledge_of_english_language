**canon** (/ˈkænən/) is the body of works, facts, or events accepted as official within a fictional universe. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/canon))

## Examples

Context: The speaker compares the current Star Wars continuity with the older **Legends** continuity.

> In both canon and legends, their home world and species name remain officially unknown.

[Why Yoda‘s Species is really SO POWERFUL — Order 77, 16:01](https://youtu.be/Dj_EeFv28Xs?t=961)

Here, **canon** is the current official Star Wars storyline. **Legends** is the banner for the older Expanded Universe stories, which were separated from the new official continuity, although creators may reuse elements from them. ([StarWars.com](https://www.starwars.com/news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page))
