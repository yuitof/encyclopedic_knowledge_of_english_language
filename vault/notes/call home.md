**call home** (/kɔːl hoʊm/) is part of the pattern **call a place home**, meaning to regard or describe a place as your home. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/call))

## Examples

The introduction contrasts Yakutsk's harsh conditions with the fact that people live there.

> The most dangerous roads on the planet. But a place that 300,000 people call home.
