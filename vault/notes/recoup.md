**recoup** (/rɪˈkuːp/) is a transitive verb meaning to recover money or costs that have been spent or lost.

Do not confuse it with **recuperate** (/rɪˈkuːpəreɪt/), whose main meaning is to regain health, strength, or energy after illness, injury, or exhaustion. **Recouperate** is a common misspelling of **recuperate**.

## Examples

Discussing an expensive Minecraft mapping project, the narrator says:

> "Even if they found all of the duped items they could ever want in the download, there would absolutely be no way to recoup the expenses of the project."
