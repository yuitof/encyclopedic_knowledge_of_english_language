**censorship** (/ˈsensəʃɪp/) is the practice of suppressing or restricting material regarded as objectionable.

Compare [[redacted]]: redaction removes or obscures particular information in a document. The terms can overlap; the reason and scope of the restriction matter.

## Examples

Dictionary example.

> They oppose government censorship.
