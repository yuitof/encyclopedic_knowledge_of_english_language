**parlaying** (/ˈpɑːrleɪɪŋ/) is [[the]] **-ing** form of **parlay**, meaning using something successfully to gain something of greater value. [[the|The]] pattern is **parlay something into something**.

## Examples

Context: [[the|The]] narration describes Huang using his influence to gain access to powerful people. [[the|The]] form heard is **parlaying**.

> And Huang may be [[even]] bigger […], parlaying influence into seats at […] exclusive tables […].
