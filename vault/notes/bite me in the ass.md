**bites me in the ass** (/baɪts miː ɪn ði æs/) is a vulgar idiom meaning that something causes me to suffer bad consequences, especially from an earlier action or mistake. ([LanGeek Dictionary](https://dictionary.langeek.co/en/word/219274))

## Examples

The Minecraft player warns that a spawning-distance problem will cause trouble later.

> It might not seem like a problem now, but I promise you this bites me in the ass later on in the video.
