**edgy** (/ˈedʒi/) is an informal adjective with two common meanings.

- **[[bold]] or provocative.** In art, fashion, or humour, it describes an unconventional style that can surprise or shock people. When used critically, it can suggest someone is trying too hard to shock people. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/edgy), [Merriam-Webster on *edgelord*](https://www.merriam-webster.com/wordplay/what-is-an-edgelord-slang-definition-words-were-watching))
- **Nervous or tense.** It describes someone who feels uneasy, especially about what might happen, or a situation where people feel that tension. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/edgy), [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/edgy))
