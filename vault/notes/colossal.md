**colossal** (/kəˈlɑːsl/) is an adjective meaning extremely large. ([OALD](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/colossal))

## Examples

In *Attack on Titan*, the name **Colossal Titan** emphasizes the Titan's enormous size. The phrase appears in this official clip's title:

> Attack on Titan - Official Clip - After 5 years the Colossal Titan Returns
