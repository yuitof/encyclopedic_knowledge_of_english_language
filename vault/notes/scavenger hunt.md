scavenger hunt
: 品揃え競争〘パーティなどで行うゲーム〙(wisdom) 👈 人を探すこともある。

「借り物競走」とかもあるかもしれないけど、運動会みたいな場でなくても scavanger hunt という単語は使えるし、「人探し」というのもチェックリストをコンプリートしていくみたいなゲーム性みたいな部分が抜けている気がする

**scavenger hunt** (/ˈskævɪndʒə ˌhʌnt/) is a game in which people or teams try to find and collect a specified set of items, usually within a time limit. ([Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/scavenger-hunt))

Countable noun; plural **scavenger hunts**.

## Examples

The players split into two teams in [[VRChat]] and race to complete a list of unusual objectives before returning to a meeting point. This version adapts the collecting game: players must find particular people or accomplish tasks, rather than simply gather physical objects.

> Today, me and my friends are going to compete in a VRChat scavenger hunt containing very absurd objectives.
