**secular Buddhism** (/ˈsekjələ ˈbʊdɪzəm/) is a modern, decentralised Buddhist movement that treats the Buddha as a human teacher and takes a naturalistic, pragmatic approach to Buddhist practice. Its adherents generally do not rely on supernatural or metaphysical doctrines such as literal rebirth, although interpretations vary; they tend to emphasise ethics, meditation, and practical change in this life.

Source: [Oxford Research Encyclopedia of Religion — Secular Buddhism](https://oxfordre.com/religion/religion/view/10.1093/acrefore/9780199340378.001.0001/acrefore-9780199340378-e-630).
