**Epstein files** (/ˈepstiːn faɪlz/) is a collective name for records connected to Jeffrey Epstein, a convicted sex offender, and investigations into his sexual abuse and trafficking of minors. These include court documents, investigative records, flight logs, and contact books.

Sources: [U.S. Department of Justice — Epstein Library](https://www.justice.gov/epstein); [DOJ — First release of Epstein files](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/attorney-general-pamela-bondi-releases-first-phase-declassified-epstein-files); [Wiktionary — Epstein pronunciation](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Epstein#Pronunciation).
