**imposter syndrome** (/ɪmˈpɒstə ˌsɪndrəʊm/), also spelled **impostor syndrome**, is a noun phrase for feeling that you do not deserve your achievements and fearing that others will discover you are less capable than they think, despite evidence of your ability.

Sources: [Cambridge — impostor syndrome](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/impostor-syndrome); [APA — impostor phenomenon](https://dictionary.apa.org/impostor-phenomenon).
