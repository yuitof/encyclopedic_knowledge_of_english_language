**particle** (/ˈpɑːtɪkl/) is in [[grammar]], a short word serving a grammatical function, often without changing its form.

In a phrasal verb, a particle such as **up** combines with the verb to help form its meaning. Compare [[sentence-final particle]]; terminology varies between grammatical frameworks.

## Examples

Dictionary example.

> The phrasal verb “look up” consists of the verb “look” and the adverbial particle “up.”
