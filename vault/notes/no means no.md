**no means no** (/noʊ miːnz noʊ/) is an expression saying that a refusal must be respected. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/no))

## Examples

A participant sets a clear limit during an online conversation.

> Nathan, no means no.
