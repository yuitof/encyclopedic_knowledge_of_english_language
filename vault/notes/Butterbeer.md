**Butterbeer** (/ˈbʌtəbɪə/) is a fictional drink in the Harry Potter stories, described as tasting like less-sweet butterscotch and served hot or cold.

In the stories it is slightly alcoholic. This fictional description should be distinguished from branded drinks sold to visitors. Related: [[The Leaky Cauldron]].
