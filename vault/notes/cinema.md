**cinema** (/ˈsɪnəmə/) is internet [[slang]] for something exceptionally entertaining, impressive, or dramatic. Often used as “absolute cinema,” it can express sincere praise or playful exaggeration.

## Examples

Context: Praising a remake of a classic [[vine|Vine]].

> Absolute cinema. That is [[the]] best one by far.
