**prosperity** (/prɒˈsperəti/) is an uncountable noun meaning the state of being successful, especially in making money; the closest common synonym is **affluence**.

## Examples

At the end of a Star Wars story, the narrator describes conditions in the restored galaxy:

> "Now, peace and prosperity had truly been restored to the galaxy."

[What if Padme Survived?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxifrStL7S0&t=1778) — Vader's Order, 29:38; watched 18 November 2026.

A prayer in another video asks for prosperity together with health:

> "May you bless all these people with abundance, prosperity, and great health, my Lord, and their family and their friends."

[DARTH VADER: THE ULTIMATE AURA FARMER (FULL SERIES)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwaxOXot17E&t=3283) — kxngmamba, 54:43; watched 18 November 2026.
