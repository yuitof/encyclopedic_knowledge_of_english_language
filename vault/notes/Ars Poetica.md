**Ars Poetica** is Horace’s Latin verse epistle on poetry and drama, also [[called]] *The Art of Poetry*. It discusses literary craft and the relationship between instruction and pleasure.

It contains the phrase [[ut pictura poesis]].

Source: [Wikipedia — Ars Poetica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ars_Poetica_(Horace)).
