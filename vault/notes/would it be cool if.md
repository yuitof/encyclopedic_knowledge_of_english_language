**would it be cool if** (/wʊd ɪt biː kuːl ɪf/) is an informal way to ask whether something would be acceptable to someone. ([Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/cool))

## Examples

Sayko asks permission to [[join]] two people. The conditional wording softens the request.

> Would it be cool if I actually joined you guys or is that is that too much?
