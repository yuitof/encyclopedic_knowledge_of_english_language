**be together** (/bi təˈɡeðə(r)/) is an expression meaning to [[be in a relationship|be in a romantic relationship]] with each other. In this sense, *together* describes two people as a couple. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary: together, sense 3](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/together_1))

Forms include **are together**, **were together**, and **have been together**. **Be together** describes an existing relationship; **get together** can describe starting one. Context matters, because *together* can also simply mean being in the same place. ([Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/together?topic=marriage-cohabitation-and-other-relationships))
