**platter** (/ˈplætər/) is the flat, circular part of a DJ turntable that supports and spins the vinyl record during playback. [Reloop’s DJ turntable guide](https://www.reloop.com/djing-with-turntables) · [Cambridge pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/platter)

![[platter-technics-sl-1200mk2.jpg|A vinyl record on a Technics SL-1200MK2, with the platter’s dotted metal rim visible beneath it]]

On this Technics SL-1200MK2, the dotted metal rim belongs to the platter. The vinyl record rests above it. Photo by Adrian DuMay, resized. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Technics_SL-1200MK2_turntable.jpg) · [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/)

Some digital DJ decks also have a **motorised platter** that spins to give DJs the feel of handling vinyl when they control a digital track. [Denon DJ](https://www.denondj.com/sc6000m-prime)

![[platter-technics-sl-dz1200.jpg|Technics SL-DZ1200 digital DJ deck with a large silver platter around its blue centre display]]

The silver circle around the display is the motorised platter on a Technics SL-DZ1200 digital DJ deck. Photo by Scott Schiller. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Technics_SL-DZ1200_Digital_DJ_Turntable.jpg) · [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) · [Technics manual](https://panasonic.ca/viewing/ALL/SL-DZ1200/OI/rqt8173-l/rqt8173-l.pdf)

## Examples

Audio-Technica’s turntable manual explains how to place a record on the deck. The spindle is the pin in the centre of the platter.

> Place the record on the platter so that the center hole aligns with the spindle.
