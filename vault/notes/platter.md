**platter** (/ˈplætər/) is the flat, circular part of a DJ turntable that supports and spins the vinyl record during playback. [Reloop’s DJ turntable guide](https://www.reloop.com/djing-with-turntables) · [Cambridge pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/platter)

Some digital DJ decks also have a **motorised platter** that spins to give DJs the feel of handling vinyl when they control a digital track. [Denon DJ](https://www.denondj.com/sc6000m-prime)

## Examples

Audio-Technica’s turntable manual explains how to place a record on the deck. The spindle is the pin in the centre of the platter.

> Place the record on the platter so that the center hole aligns with the spindle.

[Audio-Technica AT-LP120XUSB manual, “Playing a record”](https://docs.audio-technica.com/eu/AT-LP120XUSB_UM_162501380_V1_EN_web_190312.pdf)
