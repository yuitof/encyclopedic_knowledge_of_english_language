**2 + 2** is read aloud as **“two plus two.”** The full equation **2 + 2 = 4** is **“two plus two equals four”** or **“two plus two is four.”**

Because it is such a basic calculation, it can stand for something extremely simple or obvious. **What's two plus two?** asks for the answer.

Source: [Cambridge — plus](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/plus).
