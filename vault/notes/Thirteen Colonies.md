**Thirteen Colonies** (/ˌθɜːrˈtiːn ˈkɒləniːz/) were the British colonies along the Atlantic coast of North America that declared independence from Great Britain in 1776 and became the original states of the United States.

They were Connecticut, Delaware, Georgia, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, and Virginia. ([Britannica — 13 Colonies](https://kids.britannica.com/students/article/13-colonies/338325); [U.S. National Archives — Lee Resolution](https://prologue.blogs.archives.gov/2024/06/27/lee-resolution-declaring-the-thirteen-colonies-free/))
