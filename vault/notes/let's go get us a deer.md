**get** (/ɡet/) is a verb meaning to obtain something. In **get us a deer**, **us** is an indirect object meaning “for us.” ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/get))

## Examples

Janet proposes finding a deer as the team sets out to collect [[roadkill]].

> Let's go get us a deer!
