## Reference

**porch** (/pɔːtʃ/) is a covered entrance area attached to a building.

Compare [[terrace]]: an outdoor level area that need not be at an entrance. The distinction is not an absolute rule about roofs.

## Examples

Dictionary example.

> She stood in the porch and rang the doorbell.
