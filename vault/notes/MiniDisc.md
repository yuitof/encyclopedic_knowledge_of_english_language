**MiniDisc** (/ˈmɪnidɪsk/), often shortened to **MD**, is a small disc used to store and play digital audio. The 64 mm disc sits inside a protective plastic cartridge that goes into a MiniDisc player. Sony introduced the format in 1992 as a recordable alternative to audio cassette tapes.
