body scrub
: There are many different types of body scrub, but the most common are water-based, oil-based or in powder form. Body scrubs have actually been used by people for centuries and there’s evidence that ancient Egyptians used natural body scrubs to exfoliate their skin. They would use particles like sugar or salt in perfumed essential oils, which they then used on their bodies.
