**restitution** (/ˌrestɪˈtjuːʃn/) is a formal uncountable noun with two main senses:

1. The act of returning something lost or stolen to its owner.
2. Payment, usually money, for harm or loss caused to someone.

Synonyms: [restoration](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/restoration), [compensation](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/compensation), [reparation](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/reparation).
