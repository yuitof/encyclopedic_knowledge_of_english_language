**permitted** (/pəˈmɪtɪd/) is the past [[tense]] of **permit**, meaning to allow or authorize something.

The base verb **permit** (/pəˈmɪt/) has stress on the second syllable.

## Examples

Dictionary example.

> The judge permitted the release of the prisoner.
