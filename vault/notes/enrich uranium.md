**enrich uranium** (/ɪnˈrɪtʃ juˈreɪniəm/) is a verb phrase meaning to increase the proportion of uranium-235 in uranium. Here, **enrich** is a transitive verb and **uranium** is its object. The process is [[called]] **uranium enrichment**.

Sources: [Cambridge Dictionary — enrich, “ELEMENT” sense](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/enrich); [NRC — Uranium enrichment process](https://www.nrc.gov/education-regulatory-research/glossary/uranium-enrichment); [OALD — uranium, pronunciation](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/uranium).

## Examples

Cambridge Dictionary gives this example of a country’s nuclear capabilities:

> The country disclosed in 1984 that it could enrich uranium for nuclear weapons.
