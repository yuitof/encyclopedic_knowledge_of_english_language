**sterile** (/ˈsteraɪl/) is an adjective with three common meanings:

1. Free from living microorganisms, as in sterile medical equipment.
2. Unable to produce offspring, seed, or new growth.
3. Lacking vitality, creativity, or productive results.

Source: [Cambridge Dictionary — sterile](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/sterile).
