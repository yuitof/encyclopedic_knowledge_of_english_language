**custodian job** (/kʌsˈtoʊ.di.ən dʒɑb/) is a job keeping a building, such as a school or hospital, clean and in good repair. A **custodian** is the person who does that work. In North American English, **janitor** is a similar job title. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/custodian) · [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/custodian)

Typical duties include mopping floors, emptying trash, cleaning restrooms, and sometimes making minor repairs. [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/building-and-grounds-cleaning/janitors-and-building-cleaners.htm#tab-2)

## Examples

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics uses **custodians** when describing workers who maintain schools and similar buildings.

> Custodians are janitors or cleaning workers who typically maintain institutional facilities, such as schools and hospitals.
