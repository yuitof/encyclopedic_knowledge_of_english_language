**bastion** (/ˈbæstiən/) is a common name for a **bastion remnant** in *Minecraft*: a large ruined blackstone structure in the Nether, inhabited by piglins and piglin brutes. Bastions contain treasure and gold. They are separate structures from Nether fortresses.

Source: [Minecraft — Building Blocks: Bastion remnant](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/bastion-remnant).
