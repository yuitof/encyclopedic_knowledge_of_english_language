**crack** (/kræk/) can describe a voice making an abrupt, involuntary change in pitch or register. As a verb, a person's voice may **crack**; as a noun, the change itself is a **crack**.

## Examples

Introducing a singing exercise, the instructor asks:

> "Are you frustrated by a noticeable break or crack when you sing from low to high?"
