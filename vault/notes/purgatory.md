**purgatory** (/ˈpɜːɡətri/) is a noun for, in Roman Catholic belief, a state of purification after death before entering [[heaven]]. Figuratively, it can mean an unpleasant situation of suffering, often involving waiting.

Source: [OALD — purgatory](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/purgatory).

## Examples

Oxford uses the word humorously for an unpleasant routine:

> Getting up at four every morning is sheer purgatory.
