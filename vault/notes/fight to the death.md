**fight to the death** (/faɪt tə ðə deθ/) is an expression meaning to fight until one of the opponents dies. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/to%20the%20death))

## Examples

A reactor jokingly imagines a battle royale over a potential date.

> Everybody has to fight to the death.
