**youngling** (/ˈjʌŋlɪŋ/) is a young person, animal, or plant. ([Collins English Dictionary](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/youngling))

In Star Wars, it commonly refers to a child or young Jedi trainee. ([StarWars.com — What is a Padawan?](https://www.starwars.com/news/what-is-a-padawan))

## Examples

Context: Yoda is described as continuing to welcome new students with patience despite earlier failures.
