look up something
something は調べるもの。その後に前置詞句で調べる場所(インターネットか辞書とか)を示すことができる。searchとかとは逆なので注意

- A lot of the best images you can find online about [[Kowloon Walled City|the Walled City]] were taken by a photographer [[named]] Greg Girard. I **looked  him up** and found his Instagram.
