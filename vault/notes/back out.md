**back out** (/ˌbæk ˈaʊt/) is a phrasal verb meaning to withdraw from an agreement, plan, or activity, especially after having agreed to it. It can also mean to reverse out of a space.

Compare [[back up]]: **back up** generally means move backwards, support someone, or make a copy, while **back out** emphasizes leaving or withdrawing.

## Examples

The dictionary example shows withdrawal from a commitment:

> "He lost confidence and backed out of the deal at the last minute."
