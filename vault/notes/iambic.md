**iambic** (/aɪˈæmbɪk/) is an adjective describing verse built from iambs: in English accentual-syllabic metre, a weaker syllable followed by a stronger one.

**Iambic pentameter** normally has five such feet per line. [[blank verse|Blank verse]] commonly uses this metre without [[rhyme]].
