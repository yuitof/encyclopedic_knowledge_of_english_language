**PSL** (/ˌpiː ɛs ˈɛl/) is the name of an informal scale that looksmaxxing communities use to rate facial attractiveness. The initials come from three forums, **PUAHate**, **SlutHate**, and **Lookism**. [Solea and Sugiura, *Crime, Media, Culture*](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/17416590251387245)

The final letter is **L** (/ɛl/) for **Lookism**. **PSO**, with an **O** (/oʊ/), is a mix-up in this context.

A **PSL score** gives someone a rating. To [[mog]] someone is to outshine them in a direct comparison, and the person who does it is a [[mogger]].
