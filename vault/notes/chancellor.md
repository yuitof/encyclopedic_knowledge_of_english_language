**chancellor** (/ˈtʃænsələr/) is the title of the head of government in some countries, such as [[Germany]]. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/chancellor))

## Examples

In *Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith*, Mace Windu addresses Palpatine by his title as he places him under arrest. Palpatine is the **Supreme Chancellor**, the head of government of the Galactic Republic. ([Star Wars Databank](https://www.starwars.com/databank/emperor-palpatine-darth-sidious))

> In the name of the Galactic Senate of the Republic, you're under arrest, Chancellor.
