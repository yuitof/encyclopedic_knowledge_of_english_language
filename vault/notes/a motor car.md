**motor car** (/ˈməʊtə kɑː(r)/) is an old-fashioned or formal term for a car. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/motor-car))

## Comparison

The **motor** element helped distinguish an engine-powered vehicle from a horse-drawn [[carriage]]. Once **car** became the ordinary word for an automobile, speakers rarely needed the longer form. Lexical shortening is also part of the story, so the carriage distinction is a contributing explanation rather than the sole cause.

[[a motor bike|A motor bike]] remains current because **bike** can also mean a pedal-powered bicycle. Adding **motor** still does useful disambiguating work between a motorbike and a bicycle.
