Futurama

アメリカのcartoon

**Futurama** (/ˌfjuːtʃəˈræmə/) is an American animated science-fiction sitcom created by Matt Groening and developed by Groening and David X. Cohen. A pizza delivery worker named Philip J. Fry is accidentally frozen in 1999. He wakes up a thousand years later in New New York and joins the Planet Express delivery crew alongside Leela and Bender. ## Comparison with The Simpsons

*Futurama* and [[The Simpsons]] are two separate Matt Groening series, and Hulu classifies both as adult animation. *The Simpsons* uses the Simpson family and their neighbours in Springfield to satirise American family life and culture. *Futurama* uses a delivery company in the future to make jokes about work, technology, and society through stories about robots, aliens, and space travel.
