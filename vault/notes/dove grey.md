**dove grey** (/ˌdʌv ˈɡreɪ/) is a noun for a soft grey colour, sometimes with a slight pink or blue tint.

The adjective can be hyphenated before a noun: **dove-grey upholstery**, as in [[Mrs Dalloway]]. The American spelling is **dove gray**.
