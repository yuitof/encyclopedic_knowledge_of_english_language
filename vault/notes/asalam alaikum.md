**asalam alaikum** (/as.sa.laː.mu ʕa.laj.kum/) is an Arabic greeting meaning “peace be upon you,” also written **as-salamu alaykum**.

## Examples

Context: A participant greets someone during an Omegle challenge.

> Asalam alaikum, my brother.
