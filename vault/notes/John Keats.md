**John Keats** (1795–1821) was an English Romantic poet, known for his [[ode|odes]] and for *Endymion*. His poetry often explores beauty, sensation, mortality, and imagination.

Related: [[Keats House]], [[Lord Byron]], [[Lyrical Ballads]].
