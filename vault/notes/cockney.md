**cockney** (/ˈkɒkni/) is a noun for a person from London’s East End.

The word is also used in discussions of London identity and speech. The traditional birthplace criterion is being born within hearing of Bow Bells.

Source: [OALD — cockney](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/cockney).
