**imbues** (/ɪmˈbjuːz/) is the third-person singular of **imbue**, a transitive verb meaning to fill or permeate something with a feeling or quality.

Common pattern: **imbue something with something**.

## Examples

Dictionary example.

> A feeling of optimism imbues her works.
