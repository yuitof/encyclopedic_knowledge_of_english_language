**anomaly** (/əˈnɒməli/) is a countable noun meaning a person, thing, or situation that differs from what is normal or expected. Plural: **anomalies**. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/anomaly))

The word highlights an exception to a usual pattern. Whether that exception is admirable, strange, or a problem depends on the context.

## Examples

In a Star Wars parody, Luke asks about his father. The reply describes his father as an exceptionally remarkable person; *anomaly* is part of the praise, rather than a technical diagnosis.

> He was the best Jedi I've ever worked with. A true anomaly of a human.

— [If Star Wars had a $10 Budget - Full Movie — 12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0qy8Bnb9C1o&t=752s)

In a video about unusual Minecraft seeds, the narrator discusses upside-down trees caused by a world-generation bug. Here, *anomaly* refers to the trees' unexpected appearance.

> There's no real explanation for this anomaly besides a bug in the tree generation during the snapshot.

— [Minecraft's Greatest Seeds of All Time — 12:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ms1YBju63ps&t=745s)
