**sordidity** (/sɔːˈdɪdɪti/) is a rare noun for sordidness: moral baseness or degrading unpleasantness.

## Examples

In [[Mrs Dalloway]], Septimus projects his disgust with bodily life onto his reading of Shakespeare. This is his troubled interpretation, not a neutral statement of Shakespeare’s views.

> How Shakespeare loathed humanity--the putting on of clothes, the getting of children, the sordidity of the mouth and the belly!
