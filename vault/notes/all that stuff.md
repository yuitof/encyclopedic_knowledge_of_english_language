**all that stuff** (/ɔːl ðæt stʌf/) is an informal phrase referring collectively to things already mentioned or understood. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/stuff))

## Examples

A Minecraft player realizes that scattered materials need to be mined again.

> I have to mine all that stuff now?
