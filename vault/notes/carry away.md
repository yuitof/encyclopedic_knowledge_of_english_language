**carry away** (/ˌkæri əˈweɪ/) is a separable phrasal verb. It can mean to remove or transport something, while the common figurative pattern **be/get carried away** means to become so excited or absorbed that one loses restraint.

## Examples

In a conversation with James Bond, Nimi says:

> "Now, let's not get carried away, James."
