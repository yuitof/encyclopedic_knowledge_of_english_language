**Never Let Me Go** is Kazuo Ishiguro’s 2005 novel about Kathy and her memories of growing up at Hailsham. Its alternative England raises questions about friendship, memory, and what makes a human life valuable.

Related: [[The Remains of the Day]], [[The Buried Giant]].
