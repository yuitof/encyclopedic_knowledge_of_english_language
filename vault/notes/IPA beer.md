**IPA** (/ˌaɪ piː ˈeɪ/), in the phrase **IPA beer**, is an abbreviation for **India pale ale**: a pale [[ale]] with strong hop flavours and fruity notes, typically brewed with more hops—and often more alcohol—than a standard pale ale. ([Cambridge Dictionary — IPA pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/ipa); [Britannica — India pale ale](https://www.britannica.com/topic/India-pale-ale))

Here **IPA** is unrelated to the International Phonetic Alphabet covered in [[IPA]].
