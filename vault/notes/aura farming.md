**aura farming** (/ˈɔːrə ˌfɑːmɪŋ/) is internet slang for deliberately doing things that make someone appear cool, confident, stylish, or mysterious, often to gain online attention or social status. The slang sense of [[aura]] refers to perceived coolness or charisma; **farming** suggests deliberately accumulating it, typically while seeming not to try too hard.

Sources: [Merriam-Webster — aura farming](https://www.merriam-webster.com/slang/aura-farming), [Dictionary.com — aura farming](https://www.dictionary.com/culture/slang/aura-farming).
