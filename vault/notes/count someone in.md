**count someone in** (/kaʊnt ˈsʌmwʌn ɪn/) is an informal phrasal verb meaning to include someone in an activity or plan.

## Examples

One person invites another to go swimming; the reply accepts the invitation.

> “Do you want to go swimming with us tomorrow?” “Yes, count me in.”
