**benefit from** (/ˈbenɪfɪt frəm/) is a verb construction meaning to gain an advantage from something.

**Benefit from** is the usual neutral choice. The usage guide also documents **benefit by** for an advantage resulting from an action.

## Examples

Dictionary example.

> The people of Ontario and Quebec benefit economically from the abundance of hydroelectricity.
