**the States** (/ðə steɪts/) is an informal name for the United States of America. Capitalize **States** when it refers to the country. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary: state, “the States”](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/state_1))

The expression refers to the country as a whole. Common combinations include **in the States**, **from the States**, and **back to the States**.

## Examples

After learning that his conversation partner was born in Nepal, Crispy asks how long they have lived in the United States. Here, *in the States* means *in the U.S.*

> And how long have you been in the States?
