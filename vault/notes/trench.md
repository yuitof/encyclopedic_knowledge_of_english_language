**trench** (/trentʃ/) is a long narrow excavation in the ground, especially one used to shelter soldiers.

The military sense helps explain [[no-man's-land]], the area between opposing front lines.

Source: [Merriam-Webster — trench](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/trench).
