**call someone out on something** (/kɔːl ˈsʌmwʌn aʊt ɒn ˈsʌmθɪŋ/) means to criticize or challenge someone about a mistake, contradiction, or behavior, often publicly. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary — call out](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/call-out))

The object normally goes between **call** and **out**, as in **call her out on the contradiction**.

Compared with **point something out**, which simply draws attention to information, **calling someone out** usually implies criticism and personal accountability.
