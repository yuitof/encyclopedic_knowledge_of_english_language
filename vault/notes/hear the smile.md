**hear the smile** (/hɪr ðə smaɪl/) is a figurative use of **hear**, meaning to notice something through sound. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/hear))

## Examples

The speaker says the other person's voice reveals that they are smiling. The smile itself is not a sound.

> No, I can hear the smile on your face.
