**capris** (/kəˈpriːz/) is a plural noun for trousers that end below the knee but above the ankle, often around mid-calf. The longer name is **capri pants**.

Source: [Oxford Advanced American Dictionary — capris](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/american_english/capris).
