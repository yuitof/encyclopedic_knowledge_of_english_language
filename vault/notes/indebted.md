**indebted** (/ɪnˈdetɪd/) is a formal adjective meaning grateful to someone because they have helped you. The pattern is **indebted to someone for something**. The **b** is silent.

Source: [OALD — indebted](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/indebted).

## Examples

Oxford gives this expression of gratitude:

> I am deeply indebted to my family for all their help.

— [OALD — indebted](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/indebted).
