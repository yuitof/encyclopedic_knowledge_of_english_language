**hyperflip** (/ˈhaɪpə flɪp/), also written **hyper flip**, is an internet-born style or microgenre of electronic dance music. It is another name for **dariacore**, the sample-heavy mashup scene pioneered by Jane Remover’s **leroy** project in the early 2020s. Hyperflip tracks often jump rapidly between electronic and club styles, pairing recognizable samples from pop, anime, video games, J-pop, Vocaloid, and internet culture with extreme loudness, distortion, and intentional clipping. It overlaps with the wider hyperpop and internet-music scene, but it is not simply another name for hyperpop. ([Pitchfork — dariacore, also known as hyperflip](https://pitchfork.com/thepitch/remember-jane-removers-mashup-genre-dariacore-its-blowing-up-in-japan-now/); [Mixcloud — Hyperflip](https://www.mixcloud.com/blog/2026/10/01/these-are-the-genres-blowing-up-on-mixcloud-right-now/))
