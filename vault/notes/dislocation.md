**dislocation** (/ˌdɪsləʊˈkeɪʃn/) is a noun for disruption of the normal arrangement or functioning of something.

In descriptions of experience or art, a **sense of dislocation** can suggest feeling displaced or disconnected.

## Examples

Dictionary example.

> There is often a sense of dislocation in their music.
