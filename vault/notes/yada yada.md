**yada yada** (/ˈjɑːdə ˈjɑːdə/) is an informal expression used to skip details that seem obvious, repetitive, or boring. It can sound dismissive when you use it to sum up someone else’s words. It can also be written **yadda yadda** or said as **yada yada yada**. [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/yada%20yada)

## Examples

Reeon skips the familiar explanation of how the Minecraft [[mace]] works and gives only the points that matter for the chase.

> Yada yada, most powerful weapon in Minecraft. Yada yada. The more you fall, the more damage it does.
