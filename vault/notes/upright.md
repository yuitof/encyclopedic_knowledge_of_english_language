**upright** (/ˈʌpraɪt/) is an adjective that describes someone who is sitting or standing with their back straight. Common phrases include **sit upright**, **stand upright**, and **an upright posture**. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/upright_1)
