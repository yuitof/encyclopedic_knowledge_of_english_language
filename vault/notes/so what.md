**so what?** (/səʊ ˈwɒt/) is an informal response showing that you do not care about something or consider it important. It can sound dismissive or rude.

Sources: [OALD — so what?](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/so_3); [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/so-what).
