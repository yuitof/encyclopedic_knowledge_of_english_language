**the elephant in the room** (/ði ˈelɪfənt ɪn ðə ruːm/) is an obvious, significant problem that people avoid discussing. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/elephant%20in%20the%20room))

## Examples

Planning a hide-and-seek challenge, the group brings up Nick’s limited mobility.

> Can we just talk about mobility and the elephant in the room?
