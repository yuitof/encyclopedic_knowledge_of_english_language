**narrow down** (/ˌnærəʊ ˈdaʊn/) is an action phrasal verb meaning to reduce the number of possibilities or choices. It often appears in the pattern **narrow something down (to something)**.

## Examples

While discussing how genetics could identify his Minecraft parents, the narrator says:

> "Keep in mind, this is a very big oversimplification, it's very hard to narrow down who your actual parents are."
