**Keats House** is the house in Hampstead, London, formerly known as Wentworth Place, where [[John Keats]] lived in 1818–1820. It is associated with his relationship with Fanny Brawne and the composition of major poems.

Source: [Wikipedia — Keats House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keats_House).
