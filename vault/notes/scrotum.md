**scrotum** (/ˈskrəʊ.təm/) is the anatomical noun for the external pouch of skin that holds the testes in most male mammals.

![[scrotum-layers-gray1143.png|Anatomical illustration showing the layers and structures of the scrotum]]

The first diagram shows the scrotal skin, dartos, cremasteric muscle and fascia, tunica vaginalis, and the enclosed testis.

![[scrotum-cross-section-gray1145.png|Transverse anatomical section through one side of the scrotum and a testis]]

The cross-section labels the scrotal layers and internal structures of the testis.

The informal slang term is [[ball sack]].
