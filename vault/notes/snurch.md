**snurch** (/snɜːtʃ/) is informal slang meaning to steal or swipe something, especially in a casual, opportunistic, or joking way. It is associated with the science-fiction series *Farscape*, where it serves as an in-universe equivalent of “steal.”

The same spelling also appears as a humorous shortening of **snail church** or **snake church** in internet and fandom contexts. Context distinguishes the senses; neither use is standard dictionary English.

Sources: [Farscape Wiki — snurch](https://farscape.fandom.com/wiki/Snurch), [Urban Dictionary — the snurch](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=the%20snurch).
