**turn on someone** (/tɝːn ɑːn ˈsʌmwʌn/) is a phrasal verb that means to suddenly become hostile toward someone and attack or criticize them. It can suggest betrayal when that person was an ally. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/turn-on_1)

**On** introduces the person or group that becomes the target. It also marks the target in expressions such as **an attack on the temple** and **fire on those tanks**.

Compare [[迷惑の on]], where **on** can mark the person negatively affected by an event, as in [[dying on me]].

## Examples

Sidious betrays his apprentice Dooku by urging Anakin to kill him.

> The betrayal route sat open for the whole war, and in the end, the only person who ever used it was Sidious himself when he turned on Dooku.

[EVERYONE That Could Kill Emperor Palpatine, 10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OWXj6_mBhHc&t=657s)

Members of the government Obi-Wan helped form become hostile toward one another. **Turned on itself** expresses conflict within the same group.

> The government he helped build then turned on itself as friend [name unclear] started tearing down memorials by force, and Siri Tachi died trying to stop it.

[Why Luke's Lightsaber Style TERRIFIED Yoda and Obi-Wan, 15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bu8hN7nlIJE&t=958s)

Vader believes that the Jedi became his enemies.

> In Vader's mind, the Jedi turned on him.

[What If Darth Vader TRAINED Anakin Skywalker, 0:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-XVDhfVfCPw&t=44s)

The narrator describes Barriss Offee's attack on the Jedi Temple and interprets it as a rejection of what the Jedi Order had become. **On** marks the target in both uses of **attack on**.

> Her attack on the temple was an attack on what the Jedi have become, an army fighting for the dark side, fallen from the light they once held dear.

[Why Grievous Could ONLY Be Beaten by Obi-Wan, 20:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vm41P4paG4w&t=1215s)

A commander orders the cannons to shoot at the tanks. **On** marks the target of the fire.

> All cannons, fire on those tanks!

[The Republic commiting warcrimes for 8:17, 4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bcy6sSLblBM&t=266s)
