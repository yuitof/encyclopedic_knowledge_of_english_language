**descend into something** (/dɪˈsend ˌɪntə ˈsʌmθɪŋ/) means to gradually enter a bad state, such as chaos or violence.

Source: [OALD — descend into](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/descend-into).

## Examples

A student protest in Barcelona becomes violent. Article excerpt:

> […] student protest descended into what local authorities described as "gratuitous violence".

— [BBC News — France protests report, Jaroslav Lukiv](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c95ynd587ynzo).
