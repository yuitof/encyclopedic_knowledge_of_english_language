**descend into something** (/dɪˈsend ˌɪntə ˈsʌmθɪŋ/) is a formal phrasal verb meaning to gradually enter a bad state. **X** names that state, such as chaos, violence, or disorder. The article uses **descending** (/dɪˈsendɪŋ/) and **descended** (/dɪˈsendɪd/).

Source: [OALD — descend into](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/descend-into); [OALD — descend, pronunciation and forms](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/descend).

## Examples

Police deployments and restrictions aim to stop protests worsening into chaos. Article excerpt:

> descending into the kind of chaos

Here, **X = the kind of chaos** described in the report. **Prevent something from + -ing** explains the form **descending**.

The article also describes a student protest in Barcelona becoming violent. Article excerpt:

> descended into what local authorities described as "gratuitous violence"

Here, **X = what local authorities described as gratuitous violence**. **Descended** is the simple past form.
