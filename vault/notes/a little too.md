**a little too** (/ə ˈlɪtl tuː/) is a phrase meaning slightly more than is suitable or desirable. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/too))

## Examples

The narrator suggests that the timing of an event seemed suspicious.

> It looked a little too convenient.
