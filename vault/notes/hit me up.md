**hit me up** (/hɪt miː ʌp/) is an informal expression meaning contact me. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/slang/hit-me-up))

## Examples

After a brief encounter online, a speaker invites the other person to get in touch.

> Hey, hit me up.
