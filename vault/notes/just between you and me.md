**between you and I** (/bɪˈtwiːn juː ən aɪ/) is a nonstandard [[variant]] of **between you and me**, used to keep something private. In edited English, use **me** after **between**.

## Examples

Context: A speaker gives someone a heart bracelet and asks to keep the gift secret.

> That's between you and I.
