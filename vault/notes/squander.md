**squander** (/ˈskwɒndə/) is a transitive verb meaning to waste money, time, talent, or an opportunity in a careless or stupid way. It commonly appears as **squander something on somebody or something**.

Unlike [[splurge]], **squander** always conveys disapproval: the resource is not merely spent indulgently but wasted or lost to poor use.
