**on something** (/ɒn ˈsʌmθɪŋ/) is an informal predicative phrase meaning that someone is taking a drug, especially a recreational or illegal drug, or is currently experiencing its effects. It is less formal and less specific than [[under the influence]] because it does not identify the substance.

Source: [The Free Dictionary — on (something)](https://idioms.thefreedictionary.com/on+(something)).
