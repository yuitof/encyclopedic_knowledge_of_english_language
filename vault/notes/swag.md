**swag** (/swæɡ/) is slang for a stylish, confident way of dressing or carrying yourself, as in the phrase **have swag**. [Cambridge Dictionary, “swag”](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/swag?topic=fashion)

**drip** (/drɪp/) is slang for stylish clothes, shoes, or accessories. It can refer to luxury fashion, like a Gucci jacket or the Louis Vuitton outfit Travis Scott wore for *GQ*. [Cambridge Dictionary, “drip”](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/drip?topic=clothing-general-words) · [Travis Scott’s *GQ* photoshoot](https://www.gq.com/story/travis-scott-september-2020-cover-profile)

## Examples

Rapper Smino jokes about how his custom bandana gives a casual hoodie-and-boots outfit some style.

> But it’s “swag” because I’ve got the custom bust-down bandana on with it.

[Smino’s *Hypebeast* interview](https://hypebeast.com/2024/12/smino-maybe-in-nirvana-album-exclusive-interview-stream)

Crispy Concords opens a lost package and jokes that he would have found the clothing stylish as a kid.

> I mean, if I was 8 years old, that would be some drip.

[I Bought Lost Packages (Crispy Concords, 13:03)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9g8lsyiET4g&t=783s)
