**root for** (/ˈruːt fɔːr/) is an informal phrasal verb meaning to support someone or hope they succeed. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/root%20for))

## Examples

A rival thinks Chase's charming [[duck]] mascot will win him support.

> But unfortunately for me, that duck is incredibly charming and I think people are going to root for you because of it.
