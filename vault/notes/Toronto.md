**Toronto** (/təˈrɒntəʊ/) is Canada’s largest city and the capital of Ontario. It is a financial and cultural centre with a diverse population.

Sources: [OALD — Toronto](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/toronto); [Legislative Assembly of Ontario — The Capital Cities](https://www.ola.org/en/visit-learn/teach-learn-play/about-ontario/capital-cities).

**Pronunciation caution:** Put the emphasis on the second syllable: “tuh-**RON**-toh.” In casual local speech, the second **t** is often omitted, giving “tuh-**RON**-oh” (*Toronno*), sometimes shortened to “**TRON**-oh.” The first **T** remains. Pronouncing both t’s is also standard; local pronunciation varies.

Sources: [OALD — Toronto](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/toronto); [Toronto Mike — How to Pronounce Toronto](https://www.torontomike.com/2018/07/how_to_pronounce_toronto/).
