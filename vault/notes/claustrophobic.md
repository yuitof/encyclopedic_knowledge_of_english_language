**claustrophobic** (/ˌklɒstrəˈfəʊbɪk/) is an adjective describing something that creates an uncomfortable feeling of being confined.

Related: [[Claustrophobia]].

## Examples

Context: Luke reflects on spending several days living through a VR headset.

> Even though I have so much freedom, living like this feels claustrophobic.
