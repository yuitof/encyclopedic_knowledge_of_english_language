**low profile** (/ˌloʊ ˈproʊfaɪl/) is a state of attracting little public attention. To [[keep a low profile]] is to avoid drawing attention to yourself. Being [[discreet]] means acting carefully to avoid unwanted attention or keep something private. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary, “profile”](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/profile_1) · [Cambridge Dictionary, “discreet”](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/discreet)
