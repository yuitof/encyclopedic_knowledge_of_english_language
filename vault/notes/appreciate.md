**appreciate** (/əˈpriːʃieɪt/) is a verb meaning to recognize something with gratitude. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/appreciate))

## Examples

Crispy thanks viewers at the end of his [[vine|Vine]] recreation challenge.

> I appreciate you guys watching along the journey with me.

[I Recreated Popular Vines (To See If They Go Viral Again) — around 38:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4bo2Tbu5-AA&t=2293s)

## Understand or recognize

**appreciate** (/əˈpriːʃieɪt/) is also a transitive verb meaning to understand that something is true, or to recognize a situation's significance. ([Oxford Advanced American Dictionary, sense 3](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.co.uk/definition/american_english/appreciate))

Common patterns: **appreciate that + clause**, **appreciate how/what + clause**, and **appreciate + noun**. In this sense, it expresses awareness or understanding rather than thanks.

## Examples

Oxford's example concerns recognizing the seriousness of someone's illness:

> We didn't fully appreciate that he was seriously ill.

— [Oxford Advanced American Dictionary — appreciate](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.co.uk/definition/american_english/appreciate)

After playing through changing Minecraft versions, the speaker reflects on the game's development. Here, *appreciate* combines understanding its history with recognizing its value; he is not thanking someone.
