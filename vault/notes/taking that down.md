**taking that down** (/ˈteɪkɪŋ ðæt daʊn/) uses the separable phrasal verb **take down** with **that** as its object. It can mean removing or dismantling that thing, taking it offline, writing it down, or defeating it, depending on context. ([Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary — take down](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/take-down))
