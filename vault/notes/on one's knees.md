**on one's knees** (/ɑːn wʌnz niːz/) is a phrase for kneeling with both knees on the ground. **Be on one's knees** describes the position. **Get down on one's knees** describes moving into it. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/on-knees)

## Examples

One participant says he'll kneel and beg to get the other person to attempt a backflip.

> I'll beg on my knees. I need you to do a back flip.
