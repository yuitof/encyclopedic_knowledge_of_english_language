**Laocoön and His Sons** is an ancient marble sculpture depicting a Trojan priest and his sons attacked by serpents. It was rediscovered in Rome in 1506.

Literary context: the group became a central example in debates about the different expressive possibilities of poetry and sculpture, including Lessing’s *Laocoön*. Related: [[ut pictura poesis]].
