**spacing out** (/ˌspeɪsɪŋ ˈaʊt/) is the -ing form of **space out**, meaning to stop noticing what is happening around you.

Source: [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary — space out](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/space-out).

## Examples

During the [[sleep deprivation]] challenge, Luke recalls losing focus while Jordan was away.

> When you went to the bathroom, I was just kinda spacing out right here.
