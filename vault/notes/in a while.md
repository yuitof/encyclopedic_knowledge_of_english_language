**in a while** (/ɪn ə waɪl/) is a phrase referring to a period of time; after a negative verb, it means for some time. ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/while))

## Examples

The streamer says she has not recently checked her older videos.

> I haven't checked on them in a while.
