**something included** (/ˈsʌmθɪŋ ɪnˈkluːdɪd/) is a noun-plus-participle pattern meaning that an item is supplied as part of a product, package, event, or offer. It commonly appears as **something is included**, **something included with something**, or fronted **included is something**.

## Examples

While examining a sealed original iPhone, the speaker describes what was supplied with it:

> "You will find that there is a gigantic SIM card included with the iPhone."
