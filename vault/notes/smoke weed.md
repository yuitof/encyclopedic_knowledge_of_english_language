**smoke weed** (/smoʊk wiːd/) is an informal verb phrase meaning to smoke cannabis (marijuana). **Weed** is an uncountable noun in this sense. [Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary, “weed”](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/weed_1)

## Examples

Two people are talking about a dog named Indica when one asks the other whether they smoke cannabis.

> So, do you smoke weed?
