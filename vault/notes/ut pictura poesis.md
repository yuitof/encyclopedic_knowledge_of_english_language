**ut pictura poesis** is a Latin phrase meaning “as painting, so poetry.” In Horace’s [[Ars Poetica]], it compares the ways poetry and painting invite judgment and attention.

Related: [[ekphrasis]], the literary representation of visual art.

Source: [Wikipedia — ut pictura poesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ut_pictura_poesis).
