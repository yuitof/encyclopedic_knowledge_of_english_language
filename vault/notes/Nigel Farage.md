**Nigel Farage** (/ˈnaɪdʒəl ˈfærɑːʒ/) is a [[british|British]] politician known for campaigning for the United Kingdom to leave the European Union. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigel_Farage))

## Letter to Broken Britain

On 27 November 2025, Farage published the video message *My Letter to Broken Britain*. He portrays the country as economically troubled, blames Labour and the Conservatives, and presents Reform UK as an alternative for working taxpayers.

**Broken Britain** uses the image of a damaged country that can be repaired. His phrase **alarm clock Britain** refers to people who get up for work and appeals to working taxpayers.
