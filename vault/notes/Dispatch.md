**Dispatch** (/dɪˈspætʃ/) is a 2025 episodic adventure game by AdHoc Studio set in a superhero dispatch centre in Los Angeles. You play Robert Robertson, also known as Mecha Man, who takes a desk job at the Superhero Dispatch Network after his suit is destroyed. [AdHoc Studio](https://adhocstudio.com/games/dispatch)

On the city map, you decide which former villains to send to each emergency. Your dialogue choices shape Robert’s relationships with the team and how the story unfolds. [Dispatch on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2592160/Dispatch/)
