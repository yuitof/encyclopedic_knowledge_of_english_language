**Doublelift** (/ˈdʌbəlˌlɪft/) is the gaming handle of Yiliang “Peter” Peng, an American [[league of legends|League of Legends]] player and streamer. He is best known as a bot laner and one of North America’s most decorated professional players.

Peng began competitive play in 2011. Across stints with Counter Logic Gaming, Team SoloMid, Team Liquid, and 100 Thieves, he won eight League Championship Series titles, the most by any player. He has also worked as an official LCS co-streamer.
