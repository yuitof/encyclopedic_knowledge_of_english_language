**domineering** (/ˌdɒmɪˈnɪərɪŋ/) is a disapproving adjective describing someone who tries to control other people without considering their opinions or feelings. It implies an overbearing or arbitrary exercise of authority, rather than ordinary confidence or leadership.

Sources: [Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary — domineering](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/domineering), [Merriam-Webster — domineering](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/domineering).
