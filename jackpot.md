jackpot
: an impressive often unexpected success or reward

スロットマシンとかの当たりのことを jackpot と言ったりできる。

- He's won 100 jackpots and claims that he has the cheat code to turn a slot machine into an infinite money printer.

以下の例では、gold ingots を jackpots と言ってる
- There's two over there, bro. This is like the jackpot. There's another one over there, too. All right, I guess I'll [[rip-off|rip]] these guys right here. We need a soul speed book.
