nigga
: _Nigga_ originated as a variant of the infamous racial slur _nigger_, reflecting one of its pronunciations, and for many people it is an equally offensive word. In the 19th century, however, the two forms began to diverge in use among some African Americans, with _nigga_ eventually becoming the preferred term for neutral and positive self-referential uses, as well as a neutral or positive term referring to any person and often especially to a friend, partner, or spouse. Despite their prevalence in hip-hop, a highly influential music and cultural movement of African American origin, these uses of _nigga_ are themselves controversial and the use of _nigga_ by a person who is not Black—in any context—is considered highly offensive.
