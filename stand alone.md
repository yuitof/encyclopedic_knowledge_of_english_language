stand alone
: [[league|in a league of someone's own]]

- The appearance of this city has been compared to other cities like the Favelas in Rio de Janeiro for its similar chaotic self-built look or a couple of cities in Yemen for its similar rigid and compact [[morpheme|morphology]]. But as far as I'm concerned, [[Kowloon Walled City|the Walled City]] **stands alone** in its visual identity because it blends those two extremes in  a way that I don't think will ever be replicated.
